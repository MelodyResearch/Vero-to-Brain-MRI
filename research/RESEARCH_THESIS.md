# Thesis and present decision

**Question:** On genuine, patient-separated brain MRI, does task-balanced online verifiable RL improve a task-to-task transfer matrix relative to single-task and proportional-mixture RL, while preserving spatial grounding and explanation quality?

Vero demonstrates multi-category balance and negative-transfer patterns on general visual data. Med-R1, BrReMark, LoV3D, MedVol-R1 and MedGRPO already cover major ingredients. The possible contribution is a controlled empirical transfer result in MRI, or a genuinely distinct operation motivated by a reproduced failure of existing methods. Neither is established here.

**Current recommendation: execute the direct Vero-to-MRI recipe transfer in stages.** Dallas has 20 verified people, 40 sessions and 44 volumes; MSD Task04 supplies 260 mask-paired crops and a bounded MSD Task01 sample supplies eight four-sequence tumor MRI/mask pairs. Local Qwen3-VL base and released-Vero inference on the same 24 real image questions has run; the shared-template scores were 12/24 versus 9/24, respectively, with severe format and shortcut limitations. These engineering probes do not establish patient-independent performance or recipe transfer. A separate OpenNeuro `ds007045` patient-keyed 32-train/8-heldout real MRI sample is under label/task construction. The actual weight-updating GSPO arms and cross-task matrix remain next. A pretrained-checkpoint comparison measures pretrained transfer, whereas a task-mixing RL claim requires subsequent same-starting-weight training with matched budgets. Longitudinal change is one optional task-specific reward, not a global prerequisite.

**Decision gates:** Real image and mask bytes plus typed reward targets are available from several sources. Task04 patient lineage and Task01 source-case lineage remain unresolved; the current two Task04 question types share one segmentation mask. Base and released-Vero paired frozen inference are `INFERENCE_COMPLETED`; actual GSPO weight updates, a trained cross-task matrix and scientific validation remain `NOT_RUN` until verified receipts exist. Do not infer a longitudinal result from multi-frame SGMRI-VQA; its frames are within a volume rather than visits.
