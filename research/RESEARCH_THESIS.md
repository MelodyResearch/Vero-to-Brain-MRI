# Thesis and present decision

> 当前路线与实验优先级以 [CURRENT_RESEARCH_PLAN.md](CURRENT_RESEARCH_PLAN.md) 为唯一入口。本文原有阶段状态按其历史时点保留；安全、隐私、证据和人工执行授权约束继续适用。不得据历史队列文字自动启动模型或改写主计划。

**Question:** On genuine, patient-separated brain MRI, does task-balanced online verifiable RL improve a task-to-task transfer matrix relative to single-task and proportional-mixture RL, while preserving spatial grounding and explanation quality?

Vero demonstrates multi-category balance and negative-transfer patterns on general visual data. Med-R1, BrReMark, LoV3D, MedVol-R1 and MedGRPO already cover major ingredients. The possible contribution is a controlled empirical transfer result in MRI, or a genuinely distinct operation motivated by a reproduced failure of existing methods. Neither is established here.

**Current recommendation: resolve reward sparsity, then test the direct Vero-to-MRI recipe with matched effective updates.** Dallas has 20 verified people, 40 sessions and 44 volumes; MSD Task04 supplies 260 mask-paired crops, while MSD Task01 supplies eight four-sequence tumor MRI/mask pairs. A stronger OpenNeuro `ds007045` sample has 32 source training and eight heldout patients across four image-linked routes. On 107 patient-heldout prompts, frozen Qwen3-VL base and released Vero scored 45 and 44 correct, respectively; image-swap/no-image controls showed strong answer priors. A four-step SFT control changed weights but improved only one heldout grid answer relative to its matched 4-bit starting state. Strict Vero-style specialist, proportional and uniform RL each executed eight real-image rollout groups but obtained zero rewards and zero updates. An explicit post hoc single-box reward ablation produced one proportional and two uniform task-driven LoRA updates; neither improved over the same SFT start, and uniform lost one grid answer. These small Metal experiments do not establish full Vero transfer or a balancing effect. Longitudinal change remains one optional task-specific reward, not a global prerequisite.

**Decision gates:** Real image and mask bytes plus typed reward targets are available. Task04 patient lineage and Task01 source-case lineage remain unresolved; three ds007045 tumor routes share one reviewed source mask, while sequence labels come independently from BIDS sidecars. Frozen inference and limited SFT/LoRA updates are `COMPLETED`; official CUDA veRL training, matched active-update MRI task-mixture comparisons, an open-ended retention judge and scientific validation remain `NOT_RUN`. The eight-patient heldout set has been used in exploratory analysis, so further adaptive work requires a fresh final test set. Do not infer a longitudinal result from multi-frame SGMRI-VQA; its frames are within a volume rather than visits.
