## Minimum decisive controls

1. **Image conditionality, frozen before scoring.** For sequence recognition, compare the original FLAIR and contrast-enhanced T1 images from the same held-out patient under the *same neutral prompt*; the correct class should change with the pixels. Add no-image and blank-image controls. For tumor routes, swap an MRI pair with a same-site or within-patient different-slice pair chosen to have a different hidden reference while holding the question wording constant; compare answer change against no-image and blank controls. Keep all swapped images within the held-out split and never select them after seeing model predictions.
2. **Simple visual and label priors.** Report fixed majority, site-conditional training-majority, geometry-only or fixed-grid, and image-center comparators where the route permits. Evaluate label permutation during a separate *training* diagnostic, never by changing the sealed held-out gold. Inspect whether gains survive when sequence style and panel labels are controlled.
3. **Reward activity.** Log answer correctness, valid format, overlong penalties, reward ties, sample advantages, and active groups by task and patient. With the official format gate, a run that learns only output length or wrapper syntax has not learned MRI reasoning. A format-tolerant route belongs in an explicit ablation, not the faithful Vero arm.
4. **Training comparison.** Freeze the 32/8 patient split, exact prompts and evaluation before training. Run same-data SFT and every RL arm from one common checkpoint and template; separately evaluate any released-Vero initialization. Each specialist is tested on *all* held-out routes. Contrast proportional and equal-category batches with equal attempted rollout, visual-token and update budgets, and disclose unequal patient recycling. A MedGRPO calibration arm and an active-group matched arm must change only their declared mechanism.

## Decision boundaries

- **GO for engineering transfer:** actual held-out image-conditional base/Vero predictions and valid reward variance on at least the two different target-provenance families, followed by verified weight-changing MRI task-reward training from common weights.
- **DEFER scientific transfer claim:** with only eight held-out patients, repeated prompt clusters, no external source lineage, and no open-ended reference. Increase patient/site coverage and evaluate all trained arms on a larger sealed split with patient-clustered uncertainty and visual-ablation controls.
- **KILL or reformulate a route:** if same-patient image swaps do not change its predictions as the gold changes, or if a trivial majority/center/site/style comparator matches it, that route is unsuitable as evidence of image-grounded cross-task reasoning even when its programmatic answer is verifiable.
- **Novelty boundary:** successful systematic Vero-to-MRI transfer is an empirical contribution. It does not by itself establish a new RL algorithm. A new mechanism needs a reproducible direct-recipe failure plus matched calibration/filtering and same-data controls.

**Scientific significance:** presently limited to a carefully bounded patient-keyed visual-task testbed with expert-reviewed but image-derived tumor references; no biological discovery or clinical utility is established. **AI/ML significance:** the new source can support a controlled *engineering* comparison across acquisition and mask target provenance, but balanced-GSPO transfer remains unmeasured. **Experimental significance:** within-source patient-key separation is an advance over the unresolved MSD crosswalk; sample size, repeated masks, and shortcut exposure are the principal constraints.
