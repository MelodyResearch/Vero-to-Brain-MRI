# BrReMark — primary-source audit

## Verified metadata and access

Shangkun Li et al., *Enhancing Brain MRI Anomaly Detection and Reasoning with ROI Rethink and Synthetic Data*, [arXiv:2606.25894v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.25894v1), 24 June 2026. Access: full [v1 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25894v1) and HTML including Methods, Results and ablations; [author repository](https://github.com/fdu-farm/BrReMark) located. PDF page numbers count the cover as page 1 and coincide with printed pages in Tables 1–2. Repository execution and data lineage were not independently reproduced.

## Layer 1 — Research process

The targeted gap is open-ended, spatially auditable brain MRI diagnosis rather than closed-option VQA (§1). Prior medical VLMs generally answer from one pass; an answer need not identify a supporting ROI. The authors inherit Lingshu-7B as foundation, SFT, GRPO, SynthSeg, lesion masks, and LLM judging. Their distinctive construction is a two-turn hypothesis → marked 2D bounding box → re-examination trajectory (§2.1, Fig. 1), curated real/synthetic brain MRI slices, and reward gates for modality, localization, semantic response and fabricated abnormality (§§2.2–2.4, Eqs. 1–2). SFT first teaches the structured trajectory; GRPO then scores sampled responses. Synthetic pathology is deliberately restricted to RL localization, with semantic reward masked (§2.3), an attempt to prevent synthetic appearances being treated as clinical diagnoses.

The documented curation uses about 3,700 real cases across T1, contrast T1, T2, FLAIR and DWI and six abnormal datasets plus healthy IXI (§2.2). For each abnormal 3D case, the authors select a 480×480 **2D slice with maximum lesion area using the ground-truth mask**, and derive a box; normal cases use central slices. Gemini-generated trajectories and Lingshu filtering yield 12,391 RL examples (§2.2). This design makes grounding tractable but introduces oracle slice selection to the study protocol. A feasible alternative is random/standardized slice selection or full-volume search, which would test whether performance survives real localization uncertainty.

Training is reported as 8 SFT epochs then 2 GRPO epochs, 4 A100 80GB GPUs, GRPO group size 6 (§3.2). The reward uses tag score up to 0.4, localization score up to 0.5 and LLM score up to 1.0 (§2.4). Eq. 2 defines the likelihood ratio using the reference SFT policy in its denominator, while standard clipped GRPO normally uses the rollout/old policy. Whether this is a notation error or implementation choice is **NOT_VERIFIED** and matters for reproduction. No genuine longitudinal same-patient scan experiment is reported.

## Layer 3 — Next experiment

**Most consequential question.** Is performance caused by the two-turn/GRPO method or by oracle lesion-slice selection and extra synthetic supervision? Before costly training, rerun the published checkpoint on a held-out, patient-separated volume cohort under (a) mask-selected slice, (b) standardized central/atlas slice, and (c) exhaustive slice search with false-positive accounting. Primary metric: per-patient lesion detection and 3D localization at fixed false positives per scan; radiologist-reviewed lesion mask is ground truth. Then, only if robust, compare matched-data Lingshu-7B SFT-only two-turn, SFT+GRPO, and SFT+GRPO+synthetic arms. **GO** if the full-scan gain persists across sources and ablations; **REFORMULATE** if selection explains most of it; **KILL** a claimed clinical full-volume benefit if it fails on scan-level detection. Clinical diagnosis requires its own blinded endpoint.

## Independent novelty and MRI transfer

This paper already occupies brain MRI GRPO plus spatial bounding-box rewards, multi-sequence 2D examples, synthetic pathology and open-ended diagnosis. Those components cannot be claimed as new for our project. It does **not** establish a native 3D lesion search, lesion change across actual repeat visits, or balanced multi-task RL versus single-task RL. A defensible MRI extension would require voxel-coordinate evidence linked across registered visits and acquisition-aware change verification. The minimal novelty result is an improvement over a fairly adapted BrReMark on patient-held-out longitudinal localization/change while ruling out single-slice and scanner shortcuts. Merely running its two-turn reward on Dallas slices would be application, not methodological novelty.

## Missing evidence and uncertainties

Exact patient/study cross-dataset deduplication, training code-to-Eq. 2 parity, repeated-seed uncertainty, and full-volume/longitudinal behavior are **NOT_VERIFIED** from the inspected primary text. The authors do not label the selected 2D slice protocol as longitudinal.
