## Verified metadata and access

Yuxiang Lai et al., *Med-R1: Reinforcement Learning for Generalizable Medical Reasoning in Vision-Language Models*, [arXiv:2503.13939v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13939v5), dated 27 October 2025. Access: full v5 arXiv HTML, including Methods, Results and Limitations; [author repository](https://github.com/Yuxiang-Lai117/Med-R1) located. The web reader rejected the v5 PDF on size, but an independent [source reconciliation](SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md) downloaded the [exact v5 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.13939v5) and verified the key page pointers below. No independent code execution.

## Layer 1 — Research process

The paper asks whether answer-verifiable GRPO transfers medical VQA across modalities and question types and whether visible reasoning helps. Prior medical VLM adaptation is largely SFT; MedVLM-R1 had already applied GRPO to MRI→CT/X-ray (v5 §II). The authors use OmniMedVQA's open-access portion across eight modalities and five question categories, split 80/20 for each setting (§IV-A), with Qwen2-VL-2B and Qwen2.5-VL-3B as controlled base families. SFT uses next-token likelihood; GRPO inherits group-relative policy gradients, a format reward and exact option-letter accuracy reward (§III, Eq. 1). Four completions per example and one training epoch are reported (§IV-A). The authors then remove explicit thought/format output in No-Think (§III-B) and introduce answer-before-rationale Think-After (§III-C). These are meaningful design alternatives inside the paper: they test whether rewarded thought text helps or merely adds an error channel. GRPO itself and option-letter rewards are inherited; the cross-modal/task matrix and answer-order comparison are the distinctive study design.

## Layer 3 — Next experiment

**Question/hypothesis.** Is the gain from RL exploration, or from format/answer extraction and source shortcuts? On a patient- and source-separated brain MRI VQA subset, compare same-backbone SFT and GRPO with matched examples, output templates, generated-token budget and compute, across Think, No-Think and Think-After. First perform a cheap base-model prompt/answer-order evaluation; train only if that leaves a gap. Primary metric is patient-level accuracy on an external source; report paired patient bootstrap intervals, per-category scores, and rationale faithfulness from blinded counterfactual image perturbations. **GO** if RL improves external accuracy without worse grounding; **REFORMULATE** if No-Think dominates or prompts explain gains; **KILL** an explanation-focused RL claim if rationales remain non-causal.

## Independent novelty and MRI transfer

Med-R1 already covers MRI VQA with GRPO and broad medical task categories. A project claim of “GRPO for MRI” or “cross-task medical RL” is therefore redundant. The untested space is an anatomically anchored, subject-linked multi-sequence/longitudinal verifier under controlled task mixing. It requires native-volume alignment and repeated scans; this paper's 2D option-letter reward cannot be transferred unchanged. The necessary novelty experiment is a held-out subject study where the new verifier correctly rejects swapped visits or wrong-sequence evidence and improves a measured longitudinal endpoint over answer-only GRPO and SFT. If only option accuracy rises, the novelty is dataset transfer.
