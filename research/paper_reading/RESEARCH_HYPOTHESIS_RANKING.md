# Rank hypotheses by decisive, feasible tests

**Q4 — Strongest hypothesis.** The first testable scientific claim is **image-dependent paired-volume change**: for independently validated changes beyond repeatability noise, access to correctly linked, acquisition-aware prior and current brain MRI improves patient-level change judgments over current-only, metadata-only, reversed-visit and deterministic measurement controls. This is stronger as a *research starting point* than immediate balanced RL because its data and reward validity can be falsified without training. The proposed **method** claim comes later: an evidence-verified, task-balanced policy preserves spatial, quantitative, interval and report capabilities better than equal-budget simple alternatives. Existing [LoV3D](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.12071v2), [NeuroQA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.20525v1) and [BrainTRACE](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.06571v1) make the first question necessary, while [Vero](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04917v3) and [MedGRPO](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06581v4) make the second nontrivial but far from novel by default.

## What counts as a meaningful result (Q5)

**MRI science:** improvement in independently adjudicated location or repeatability-qualified change on held-out people, with sequence/region/laterality evidence, calibrated abstention and tolerable false assertions. A positive FreeSurfer-derived scalar alone does not prove biological progression; a report-like explanation alone does not prove clinical accuracy. **AI/ML:** a repeated, patient-independent interaction between task training and transfer that persists beyond matched SFT, data exposure, proportional sampling and reward/active-group calibration, with an ablation locating the mechanism. **Experimental design:** subject/source separation, fixed image evidence and evaluator, registered physical coordinates, independent target, equal training and visual budgets, seeds, patient-cluster uncertainty and every prespecified task reported. [BrainTRACE Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.06571v1) illustrates why open-ended judge sensitivity must be reported; [NeuroQA Tables 2–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.20525v1) illustrates why a no-image/text-only floor must be beaten.
