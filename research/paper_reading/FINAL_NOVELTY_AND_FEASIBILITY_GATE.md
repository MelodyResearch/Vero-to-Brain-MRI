# Reformulate MRI RL around verified change

**Decision: REFORMULATE the research claim; GO for the next real-data verifier and frozen-model experiment.** A direct Vero-style recipe transfer is technically plausible, but its optimizer, category balancing and routed rewards are inherited. The reviewed literature already contains MRI VQA with GRPO, selected-slice brain-MRI grounding with RL, multi-task medical RL, longitudinal T1 verification with DPO, and paired-scan MRI benchmarks. A publishable candidate is an **acquisition-aware, image-dependent, patient-linked change verifier** whose measured errors and training benefit survive strong SFT, simple measurement and RL calibration controls. That candidate is **NOT_YET_DEMONSTRATED** and its global literature novelty is **NOT_VERIFIED**. The project's real Dallas pilot verified eight NIfTI volumes from two subjects and rendered them on CPU; it did not run VLM inference or RL and cannot estimate clinical or patient-population performance ([execution status](../../results/EXECUTION_STATUS.md), [lineage](../../data/SUBJECT_STUDY_LINEAGE.md)).

This is the answer to **“has the scientific problem been solved?”**: the *pieces* exist and some longitudinal evaluation already exists; the controlled causal question of image-dependent, acquisition-robust paired change with balanced RL has **not been established by this inspected set**. Absence in 13 papers is not proof of worldwide firstness. The [cross-paper matrix](CROSS_PAPER_METHOD_MATRIX.md) gives all 13 and their exact limits.

## Q2–Q3: Vero transfers as an experimental scaffold, while MRI truth must be built

Vero's sample metadata and reward-router interface, group rollout logging, inherited GSPO implementation and matched single-task/mixed comparison can transfer as **baseline scaffolding** ([Vero §3.3 and Fig. 7](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917v3); [data schema](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero/blob/main/docs/DATA.md)). Its uniform six-category weighting is only a tested *general-visual* arm, not a default MRI optimum. String/option/format rewards can check output syntax but cannot establish tissue change. MRI-specific logic must verify subject identity, visit order, sequence/protocol, affine and physical coordinates, region correspondence after QC registration, magnitude relative to repeatability error, uncertainty/abstention, and unsupported-report claims. A genuinely new method claim would need to show that this verifier adds a valid capability that [LoV3D](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.12071v2), [BrReMark](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25894v1), [NeuroQA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.20525v1)-style SFT, and deterministic measurement rules cannot provide under equal allowed evidence. The candidate is **verification of paired physical-space change and preservation of other MRI tasks**, not GSPO, “MRI + GRPO,” generic balance, or a 2D IoU route. See the [transfer audit](VERO_TO_MRI_TRANSFER_AUDIT.md) and [gap tests](UNRESOLVED_RESEARCH_GAPS.md).

## Q4–Q5: Make the image and independent endpoint decisive

The strongest near-term hypothesis is that a **correctly linked prior scan changes a patient-level decision** when the target change is above a prespecified repeatability limit; a reversed or wrong-person prior must not provide the same benefit. Run this as a frozen-model and deterministic-measurement experiment **before any RL**. Select a larger, rights-cleared cohort of true repeat 3D T1 scans with visit/acquisition metadata, subject-disjoint train/validation/test, independently QC'd longitudinal region or lesion references, and a sealed test. Add FLAIR only where cross-visit acquisition and reference validity support it. Dallas's one FLAIR pair changes through-plane spacing from 6 to 2.5 mm, which makes it a useful acquisition-shift stress case, not a biological change truth ([lineage](../../data/SUBJECT_STUDY_LINEAGE.md)). The blinded verifier challenge should include reversed order, wrong person, wrong sequence, left/right flip, registration error, protocol shift, stable repeat and fabricated measurement. **Failure on these challenges kills the proposed longitudinal reward**, even if a model obtains high answer accuracy.

Only after that gate should training compare **base; same-data and format-matched SFT; single-task RL for each supported task; proportional multi-task RL; uniform multi-task RL; uniform plus faithful MedGRPO-style nonlinear calibration and tied-group handling; and a deterministic measurement-rule baseline**. Hold starting weights, unique-patient exposure, output schema, images/renderer, visual-token and generated-token budgets, rollouts, updates, optimizer settings and evaluator fixed; repeat seeds. Record the full train-task × test-task matrix, active/nonzero-variance groups, reward errors, subject-cluster uncertainty, per-site/protocol strata, abstentions and open-ended factual regressions. A balance advantage over an unnormalized broken run alone is insufficient. For exact per-prompt group-standardized advantages, positive affine reward scaling is invariant when epsilon is negligible; MedGRPO's Table 3 does not identify *raw scale* alone as the cause of collapse ([Eqs. 1–3/Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06581v4); [statistical review](STATISTICAL_SCIENTIFIC_SIGNIFICANCE_REVIEW.md)).

**Scientific significance** would be fewer independently adjudicated spatial or interval errors per patient, with acceptable false assertions and abstention under acquisition variation. **AI/ML significance** would be a repeated, controlled negative-transfer pattern and protection beyond SFT, proportional sampling, generic reward calibration and active-group filtering. **Experimental significance** means no patient/source leakage, the same allowed evidence and compute, an independent reference and blinded reporting of every prespecified task. A higher benchmark average or prettier generated report alone meets none of those thresholds. [BrainTRACE Appendix C.5–C.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.06571v1) documents judge disagreement; [NeuroQA Tables 2–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.20525v1) shows the importance of text-only controls.

## Ten decision answers

| User decision question | Evidence-linked answer |
|---|---|
| **1. Is direct Vero-style training-recipe transfer technically feasible?** | **Conditionally yes** as a software and optimizer experiment once a compatible model, compute, rights-cleared patient cohort, valid rewards and held-out references exist. It has **not been run locally**. [Vero repository](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero); [local status](../../results/EXECUTION_STATUS.md). |
| **2. Which components transfer unchanged?** | Metadata-routed task dispatch, standardized answer parsing where appropriate, group rollout/GSPO baseline code, budget logging and single-versus-mixed design. “Unchanged” here is an implementation possibility, **not validated performance transfer**. [Vero §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917v3). |
| **3. Which require MRI modifications?** | Spatial/numeric/change truth, coordinates and registration, sequence and acquisition identity, independent reference, repeatability threshold, abstention, false-claim judge and patient split. [LoV3D §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.12071v2); [BrReMark §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25894v1). |
| **4. Already solved?** | Broad combinations are occupied, including MRI RL and longitudinal verifier training, but the controlled paired-change plus balanced-transfer test is **not established in these sources**; worldwide novelty remains `NOT_VERIFIED`. [Matrix](CROSS_PAPER_METHOD_MATRIX.md). |
| **5. Exact methodological novelty remaining?** | **Candidate only:** an independently validated, acquisition/repeatability-aware paired physical-space evidence verifier plus a demonstrable advantage over closest prior and simple controls. If only existing routes are relabeled, it is an application. [LoV3D](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.12071v2); [Vero](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04917v3). |
| **6. Minimum real-data experiment?** | A sealed subject-disjoint repeat-T1 cohort with independent change target; challenge the verifier and frozen model using true/reversed/wrong-person/current-only/no-image inputs plus deterministic measurement. No RL is needed for this first falsifier. [Hypothesis ranking](RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS_RANKING.md). |
| **7. Required baselines and ablations?** | Same-data SFT, deterministic measurement, metadata/current-only, answer-only reward, single-task RL, proportional and uniform mix, MedGRPO-style calibration, active-group/tie handling, open-ended retention, visit/subject/sequence/geometry swaps; equal budgets and seeds. [Vero Table 4/Fig. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04917v3); [MedGRPO Table 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06581v4). |
| **8. What demonstrates negative transfer?** | A prespecified, repeatable patient-level off-diagonal decline in the train-task × test-task matrix after a single-task update, relative to the same starting model, with clustered uncertainty and seeds; not simply a cross-paper score difference. [Vero Fig. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04917v3). |
| **9. What establishes balanced multi-task benefit?** | A prespecified patient macro and worst-task improvement with no material report/grounding regression, beyond same-data SFT, proportional mixture, faithful calibration and active-group controls across seeds/sites. A different Vero checkpoint is not an MRI RL experiment. [Vero Table 2/Fig. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04917v3); [MedGRPO Table 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06581v4). |
| **10. What makes us abandon/reformulate?** | **KILL the specific reward** if it accepts swapped/wrong-person evidence or unsupported change. **KILL the distinct balance-method claim** if strong SFT/proportional/calibration controls match it or patient-cluster uncertainty excludes the preregistered meaningful margin. **REFORMULATE** to benchmark/verifier work if labels or acquisition make change unresolvable. [Vero §6](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917v3); [LoV3D limitations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.12071v2). |

## Independent-review disagreement and reproducibility status

Claude Code's [raw six-paper review](CLAUDE_INDEPENDENT_METHOD_REVIEW_RAW.md) was genuinely independent but limited to summarizing HTML fetches, without PDF pages or most code. It recommended **REFORMULATE** and correctly challenged uniform mixing, SFT comparators, judge circularity and patient splits. Two claims needed correction: (i) its “reward scale first-order” explanation is not isolated under exact GRPO group standardization, although [MedGRPO Table 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06581v4) reports a real implementation collapse; (ii) the claimed absence of code links reflected its search scope, since official [Med-R1](https://github.com/Yuxiang-Lai117/Med-R1), [MedGRPO inference](https://github.com/UII-AI/MedGRPO-Code), [BrReMark](https://github.com/fdu-farm/BrReMark), [LoV3D](https://github.com/Anonymous-TEVC/LoV-3D) and [MedVol-R1](https://github.com/AdvancingEther/MedVol-R1) repositories were located. Its statement that none of the six papers measures reward hacking also conflicts with [Vero §6](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917v3), which explicitly describes observed judge gaming and fabricated measurement details. These corrections do **not** invalidate Claude's broader caution that MRI reward hacking remains untested. The [novelty skeptic](NOVELTY_SKEPTIC_REVIEW.md) and [statistical reviewer](STATISTICAL_SCIENTIFIC_SIGNIFICANCE_REVIEW.md) independently converged on verifier/image-use gates; neither reviewer reproduced paper code or trained our model.

Source-level mismatches remain open: Med-R1 PDF 328×328 versus current README 384×384; NeuroQA v1 expert count and text-only margin versus current README; Vero paper judge versus current training guide; BrainTRACE paper dataset account and primary `gpt-4o` judge versus current code defaults. These are [documented with exact pointers](SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md). Any benchmark or method reproduction must pin paper version, code commit, data revision, checkpoint, preprocessing and judge model before comparison. All cited paper outcomes are **published reports**; the local eight-volume pilot is **CPU-tested feasibility**; MRI VLM inference, reward challenge and RL outcomes remain `NOT_RUN`.
