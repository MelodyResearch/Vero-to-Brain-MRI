# NV-Reason-CT — native 3D CT tokens and finding-set GRPO

## Verified metadata and access

Andriy Myronenko et al., *NV-Reason-CT: 3D Visual Language Model for CT Analysis*, [arXiv:2609.27511v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.27511), dated 25 September 2026. Full 49-page PDF, including examples and limitations, checked; [training code](https://github.com/NVIDIA-Medtech/NV-Reason-CT) and [weights](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NV-Reason-CT) are linked by the paper. Their executable behavior and weights were **NOT_VERIFIED** here. Domain is chest/abdominal CT, not brain MRI or repeat visits.

## Layer 1 — Research process

The authors target the spatial information loss of 2D-slice approaches and flattened 3D visual embeddings. A Primus 3D ViT initialized from COLIPRI produces a 24³ grid (13,824 tokens), all passed through a projector to Qwen3.5-4B; depth, height and width indices enter the language model through multimodal rotary positions (Methods Sec. 3.2, Fig. 2, PDF pp. 4–5). They jointly fine-tune encoder, projector and LLM on reports, QA, expert and report-derived synthetic reasoning, dialogues and refusals (Sec. 3.6). GRPO follows SFT; 16 responses per prompt are rewarded by `2 × finding-set F1 + 0.5 × structural-format + length penalty`, with no explicit reference-model KL in the reported run (Secs. 3.7–3.8, PDF pp. 9–10). The finding set is parsed from an `<answer>` block; report prose correctness is **not** itself checked by that format reward. Training uses 16 × 8 H100 GPUs (Sec. 3.9, PDF p. 10).

CT-RATE, CancerVerse and internal NIH CT form training sources; the CT-RATE split is stated to be patient-disjoint (20,000 train, 1,304 validation patients), and NIH reports unique patients per case (Table 1, PDF p. 12). Those are cohort-specific statements, not proof of disjoint identity across all sources. No longitudinal visit pairing is systematically used; the paper says prior exams are a limitation (Sec. 6, PDF p. 33).

## Layer 2 — Evidence audit

- **Direct Evidence, reported performance:** CT-RATE generated answers reach macro-F1 0.614 and AUROC 0.871 for abnormalities; report-derived labels from generated reports reach macro-F1 0.592 (Results Sec. 5.1 and Fig. 6, PDF pp. 16–21). The system has no task-specific classification head for this assessment.
- **Conditional Evidence:** External and internal CT cohorts broaden evaluation, but label ontologies, unit of evaluation and prompting differ among compared methods; authors detail those differences in Sec. 5.1.1 (PDF pp. 18–21). Report-label agreement is not direct radiologist adjudication of factual prose.
- **Not Established:** A controlled SFT-only versus SFT+GRPO, or spatial-grid versus merged-token, or expert-reasoning versus no-reasoning ablation is not presented as a decisive matched comparison; the authors explicitly request such ablations in Sec. 6 (PDF p. 33). Therefore performance cannot be assigned specifically to GRPO or the 3D positional interface.
- **Conditional Evidence:** Fig. 10 reports mean reader time 26.25→13.13 minutes with AI (PDF pp. 31–32). The study always placed unaided review first, so recall and order confound the 50% figure; it is a preliminary perception study, not a clinical efficacy trial (Sec. 6, PDF p. 33).

## Layer 3 — Next experiment

For the CT authors' core mechanism, start with one patient-disjoint set and the same SFT checkpoint; compare no-RL with GRPO under matched generation and compute, then compare full 3D coordinates with shuffled/deleted coordinates. Use pre-registered finding F1, laterality errors and blinded factual-report review; cluster uncertainty by patient. If GRPO or coordinates yield no incremental benefit, the attributed mechanism is falsified even if the final model is strong. For MRI transfer, require MRI-specific preprocessing, native 3D volumes, repeated visit linkage and an independent interval-change reference before any clinical claim. A CT-only model trained on static crops cannot establish longitudinal reasoning.

**Decision:** REFORMULATE for this project's MRI goal. The representation is a technically distinct native-3D precedent, but its compute footprint is far beyond this project's CPU pilot and does not solve temporal verification.

## Independent novelty and MRI transfer

Native 3D positional tokens and finding-set GRPO are inherited prior art for any proposed MRI architecture/reward. A genuine new result would have to beat a matched native-3D SFT control on *patient-disjoint longitudinal MRI change* with visual, prior-scan and no-image ablations. Merely changing CT to MRI is application transfer. MRI intensity is non-quantitative and acquisition-dependent, and patient change is an interval property that the present reward does not encode.

## Missing evidence and uncertainties

No local code execution, reproduction, model-weight check or cross-dataset identity audit was performed. No claim here that the reported reasoning text reflects internal computation; the authors caution against that interpretation (Sec. 6).
