# MedMO — multi-domain 2D medical grounding with GRPO

## Verified metadata and access

Ankan Deria et al., *MedMO: Grounding and Understanding Multimodal Large Language Model for Medical Images*, [arXiv:2602.06965v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.06965), 11 March 2026. Full 22-page PDF including Appendices A–E checked. The paper links [code](https://github.com/genmilab/MedMO) and model collection; executable behavior was **NOT_VERIFIED**. It studies broad 2D medical image/QA/report/box tasks; its MedSG object-tracking benchmark must not be mistaken for registered repeat brain MRI.

## Layer 1 — Research process

Starting from Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct, the authors stage large general medical SFT (18.5 million image-text pairs at 768²), high-resolution/grounding SFT (3 million at 1280²), instruction tuning (4.3 million), then GRPO on 300,000 samples (Methods Fig. 2, PDF pp. 2–4). This was a 64 × MI210 GPU, 25-day training process (Sec. 4.1, PDF p. 5). Reward combines label accuracy, bounding box, tag count and overlap penalty (Sec. 3.5, PDF p. 5). The box component uses Hungarian matching on L1 and GIoU and coverage-normalized quality (Eqs. 7–12, Appendix A, PDF pp. 16–17). Appendix A derives image dimensions from box extrema when necessary and sets default false-positive/false-negative penalties to zero; those choices matter when adapting a reward to MRI geometry.

## Layer 2 — Evidence audit

- **Direct Evidence, narrow:** Table 4 (PDF p. 9) reports box IoU before→after RL of 8.8→13.3 on NIH, 38.5→38.9 on DeepLesion, and 54.6→55.0 on Bacteria. The latter two increments are only +0.4 point. This is evidence for the reported checkpoint comparison, not a large general effect of box reward.
- **Conditional Evidence:** Broad VQA, text QA, report and grounding tables compare models with different pretraining corpora and budgets (Tables 1–3, PDF pp. 5–8). Stage-wise Figs. 5–6 (PDF p. 8) show gradual changes, with the largest gains from high-resolution SFT; they do not cleanly isolate GRPO for all tasks. The authors acknowledge task-level forgetting (Conclusion, PDF p. 9).
- **Not Established:** No patient-separated repeated brain-MRI scan analysis, quantitative longitudinal reference, native 3D mask/volume metric, or clinical change adjudication is reported. MedSG multi-view or object-tracking scores describe that benchmark's images; they are not evidence of radiological temporal progression.
- **Independent concern:** The abstract's large grounding comparison against baseline differs from the small *within-model post-RL* Table 4 deltas. Use Table 4 to assess the incremental reward, not a cross-model headline to imply the reward caused the entire gain.

## Layer 3 — Next experiment

Using training-only brain-MRI images with verified boxes and physical frame metadata, compare the same checkpoint with standard coordinate-aware IoU reward versus the paper's GIoU/L1 reward, holding rollouts, prompts, updates and supervision fixed. Evaluate frame IoU, false extra boxes and laterality on patient-disjoint scans, including boxes near frame edges and no-lesion images. If the specialized reward gives no advantage over ordinary IoU under matched compute, its MRI adaptation has no demonstrated gain. A second, separate experiment with true repeat visits would be needed for longitudinal claims; static box scores cannot falsify temporal reasoning.

**Decision:** REFORMULATE for MRI. Spatial reward is transferable only after authoritative frame coordinates and reference boxes are available.

## Independent novelty and MRI transfer

Medical box-matching reward, GRPO and multimodal task mixture are established by this paper. The possible MRI difference is explicit physical-coordinate and cross-visit verification, not the existence of a bounding-box reward. Patient-level leakage controls and realistic 3D/temporal references would be needed to show anything beyond application of MedMO-style grounding.

## Missing evidence and uncertainties

No local code or checkpoint run, independent patient split audit, multi-seed estimate or clinical reading study was verified. Box reward behavior on MRI voxels is an inference, not paper evidence.

Scientific Question: does specialized GIoU/L1 reward improve MRI localization over plain IoU at equal budget? Falsifiable Hypothesis: physical-coordinate-consistent matching reduces false boxes and laterality errors on held-out subjects. Potential Method: MRI-aware frame-to-world box verifier. Minimal Experiment: same checkpoint/data with SFT continuation, plain IoU RL and MedMO-style matching RL. Strongest Baselines: equal-budget SFT and plain IoU RL.
