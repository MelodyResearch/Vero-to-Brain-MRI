# Transfer Vero's controls, rebuild MRI verification

**Q2 — What Vero contributes.** [Vero v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917v3) is an open recipe for broad visual RL: it curates six roughly equal general-image categories from 59 datasets, assigns a metadata `reward_type` to ten answer verifiers, uses inherited GSPO/sequence-ratio optimization with group-relative advantage, and explicitly includes caption/instruction tasks to protect open-ended behavior (§§3–6; [PDF pp. 4–14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.04917v3); [data schema](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero/blob/main/docs/DATA.md)). **GSPO is inherited**, as are numeric/string/box verifiers in isolation. The useful transferable contribution is the *experimental recipe*: explicit task and verifier routing, matched single-task versus mixed arms, off-target transfer matrix, and open-ended retention. No Vero experiment establishes MRI patient change, native 3D localization, cross-sequence registration or an optimal MRI category ratio.

## The reward-scale dispute has a mathematical boundary

[MedGRPO v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06581v4) reports that omitting its dataset-task logistic transform produces a severe collapse (Table 3, [PDF p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06581v4)). Claude's [independent, source-limited review](CLAUDE_INDEPENDENT_METHOD_REVIEW_RAW.md) interpreted this as proof that *raw reward-scale imbalance is first-order and sampling weights second-order*. That causal ranking is **not established** by the ablation. For the standard within-prompt advantage `A_i=(r_i−mean(r))/(std(r)+ε)`, a positive affine change `r'_i=a r_i+b`, `a>0`, gives the same `A_i` when `ε=0`; with fixed nonzero `ε`, only the denominator's epsilon term differs. A purely per-task positive scale/offset cannot rebalance tasks under exact per-group standardization. This is a mathematical deduction from group normalization, not a replicated MedGRPO training result. A nonlinear logistic mapping can alter **within-group margins** for three or more distinct rewards, but it cannot split an all-tied group; for two non-tied samples standardized signs remain the same when epsilon is negligible. Clipping, reward mixing, omitted normalization, task weights, active-group filtering and numeric behavior can change the actual update. The paper reports a real result under its implementation; its mechanism remains underidentified. The necessary cheap test is a logged rollout replay comparing raw, positive affine, logistic, rank/quantile and tied-group filtering on *identical* groups before any MRI training. See [MedGRPO Eqs. 1–3/Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06581v4) and the [independent statistical analysis](STATISTICAL_SCIENTIFIC_SIGNIFICANCE_REVIEW.md).

## The MRI verifier must represent evidence, not format

A candidate longitudinal claim should identify **the same person**, two correctly ordered visits, the same anatomical region in registered physical coordinates, each sequence's acquisition properties, a separately QC'd reference change and its test–retest or segmentation uncertainty. A reward can then accept a supported increase/decrease, reject a wrong region/laterality/visit, and give an explicit *indeterminate* outcome when the observed difference is beneath a prespecified repeatability bound. This is a **proposed mechanism, not an implemented or clinically validated verifier**. LoV3D already uses FreeSurfer-derived normative regions and longitudinal consistency with DPO ([§2/Table 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.12071v2)); BrReMark already rewards MRI boxes on a mask-selected slice ([§§2–3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25894v1)); MedVol-R1 already rewards CT slice boxes and local mask Dice ([§2/Table 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.26621v1)). Novelty therefore depends on independently verified *paired physical-space change* and a measured capability advantage over these ancestors, not the presence of a new `reward_type` string.

The challenge set precedes optimizer work: correct pair, visit reversal, wrong-person prior, wrong sequence, left/right flip, coordinate offset, misregistration, acquisition-shifted repeat, stable scan, and fabricated abnormality. Preserve normal cases and abstentions. Report false acceptance of wrong evidence and true acceptance of supported change per patient, stratified by protocol. Any reward that approves swapped or wrong-person evidence is unsuitable for longitudinal RL. For training, use the same starting checkpoint and real, subject-separated MRI data across same-data SFT, single-task RL, proportional mixture, uniform mixture, calibrated mixture and open-ended-category ablations. Log per-task reward distributions, ties, informative groups, format compliance, false assertions and independent patient endpoints. Vero Fig. 7 provides a general-visual transfer precedent; [MedGRPO Table 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06581v4) shows that one task can regress in a medical mixture. Neither is an MRI result.
