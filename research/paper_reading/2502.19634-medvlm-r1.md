# MedVLM-R1 — primary-source audit

## Verified metadata and access

Jiazhen Pan et al., *MedVLM-R1: Incentivizing Medical Reasoning Capability of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) via Reinforcement Learning*, [arXiv:2502.19634v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.19634v2), dated 19 March 2025. Access: full [v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.19634v2) and HTML, including Methods, Results and Limitations; [author code](https://github.com/JZPeterPan/MedVLM-R1) and [inference checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/JZPeterPan/MedVLM-R1) located. PDF page numbers below count the cover as page 1; printed page numbers coincide in the cited table. Code was located, not reproduced.

## Layer 1 — Research process

The authors seek accurate, visibly explained radiology multiple-choice VQA with few medical training examples. They describe answer-only SFT, expensive teacher rationale distillation, and learned-reward RL as antecedents (§§1–2). Their design choice is Qwen2-VL-2B plus inherited GRPO, six completions per prompt, and a rule reward for exact answer and `<think>/<answer>` formatting (§3, Eq. 3.1). This avoids reference rationales and a critic/reward model. The contribution is its medical adaptation, reward/prompt specification and MRI-to-CT/X-ray evaluation; GRPO, the base VLM and XML-style formatting are inherited. Their documented rationale for choosing GRPO is lower resource use and answer-only labels. An equally plausible alternative was identical data and backbone with answer-only SFT, or SFT with a matched reasoning prompt. The paper includes the first SFT comparison but changes the evaluation prompt (§4), leaving prompt interaction partly unresolved.

The training set is 600 MRI question-image pairs; testing uses 300 each from MRI, CT and X-ray, drawn from a processed HuatuoGPT-Vision VQA collection (§4 Dataset). Training is reported as 300 steps, batch size 2, six candidates, on two A100 80GB GPUs for about four hours (§4 Implementation). The explicit optimization target checks answer/format, **not** factual validity or causal faithfulness of the rationale (§3 Reward).

The authors' own §5 Limitations states pathology/OCT failures, dependence on closed choices and hallucinated reasoning. The 600-pair training and 300-per-modality test design also limits precision of generalization claims. No longitudinal evaluation or full-volume MRI evidence is present.

## Layer 3 — Next experiment

**Question/hypothesis.** Does answer-reward GRPO improve held-out radiology VQA beyond data, format and prompt effects? Freeze the same Qwen2-VL-2B starting checkpoint, the same 600 patient-disjoint training items and a source-disjoint test; compare (i) base, (ii) answer-only SFT, (iii) format-matched SFT, (iv) answer-only GRPO, and (v) format-plus-answer GRPO at matched update/token budget. Use patient or source as the bootstrap and randomization unit; report per-modality accuracy and abstention calibration. A simple prompt audit should precede training. **GO** if GRPO gains persist across seeds and sources with no rationale-faithfulness regression; **REFORMULATE** if prompt/format explains the effect; **KILL** this mechanism if gains vanish under the matched test. Expert review of explanations is a separate endpoint, not implied by answer accuracy.

## Independent novelty and MRI transfer

The exact novelty is small-data medical GRPO applied to 2D closed-choice radiology VQA. Brain-MRI RL, by itself, is therefore not novel; neither is rewarding a correct option. For real 3D longitudinal MRI, the answer verifier must instead be tied to a native-volume measurement, spatial reference and subject/visit lineage. A minimal genuinely new transfer test would compare a volume-aware paired-visit verifier against this paper's answer-only reward on held-out subjects, with correct-vs-swapped visit and cross-sequence perturbations. If it cannot discriminate those perturbations, the transfer does not establish longitudinal reasoning.

## Missing evidence and uncertainties

Source/patient deduplication, multi-seed uncertainty, compute-matched prompt controls, and independent rationale-faithfulness validation are **NOT_VERIFIED**. A single test table cannot establish full-volume or longitudinal clinical transfer.
