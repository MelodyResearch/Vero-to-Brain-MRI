# MedGRPO — primary-source audit

## Verified metadata and access

Yuhao Su et al., *MedGRPO: Multi-Task Reinforcement Learning for Heterogeneous Medical Video Understanding*, [arXiv:2512.06581v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06581v4), dated 8 April 2026; the author's [project page](https://yuhaosu.github.io/MedGRPO/) and [released inference repository](https://github.com/UII-AI/MedGRPO-Code) were located. Access: full [v4 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.06581v4) and HTML, including Methods, Results, ablation tables and appendices. The located repository explicitly contains reference **inference**, not a verified training implementation. PDF page numbers count the cover as page 1; cited tables are on PDF p. 7.

## Layer 1 — Research process

The authors target heterogeneous medical video tasks, whose response formats and evaluation scales differ (§§1–2). Their first contribution is MedVidBench: 531,850 video-instruction pairs from eight sources and eight tasks; a smaller 51,505-example Standard version balances tasks (§3.1, §4.1). SFT on Qwen2.5-VL-7B establishes medical instruction following and reward-distribution percentiles; inherited GRPO then updates from that checkpoint (§3.2). They propose a dataset-task logistic transformation of raw metric x, using the SFT median and IQR, before per-prompt group-relative advantage (§3.3, Eqs. 1–3). A GPT-4.1-based comparative judge scores five caption dimensions, combined equally with semantic similarity (§3.4). The architecture and GRPO optimizer are inherited; benchmark construction, percentile-calibrated reward and medical judge are the paper's specified additions. Alternatives include pure SFT, ordinary reward scaling, rank transforms, advantage clipping, prompt sampling reweighting, or non-RL balanced multitask fine-tuning.

The Standard split is 45,260 train/6,245 test across 611 videos (§4.1). Appendix C reports 8 H100 GPUs for SFT and GRPO, group size eight, GRPO learning rate 5e-7, and no KL penalty. The main §3.2 gives asymmetric clipping 0.2/0.3 while Appendix C states 0.2/0.28: exact implemented setting is **NOT_VERIFIED**. Patient/video identity split assurance across eight sources, multi-seed intervals, and training code are **NOT_VERIFIED**. These are medical **videos**, mostly surgery/procedure tasks, not brain MRI volumes or repeat visits.

**Mechanism check (independent derivation).** For all G outputs from one prompt, if transformed reward is a·r+b with a>0, then `(a·r_i+b−mean(a·r+b))/std(a·r+b)` equals the original normalized advantage (apart from epsilon/numerical handling). Thus mere dataset-specific affine reward scale is not sufficient to explain Table 3 Row B under the paper's Eq. 1. A logistic transform can change the standardized vector because it is nonlinear. The reported collapse is real as a result, but the proposed cross-dataset *scale* mechanism is an **Author Interpretation** rather than a cleanly isolated cause.

## Layer 3 — Next experiment

**Hypothesis.** The logistic reward's benefit comes from nonlinear within-group calibration or degenerate-group handling, not cross-dataset scale alignment alone. Use the same SFT checkpoint, frozen dataset/order/rollouts where possible, and equal update/compute budget for (i) raw reward, (ii) dataset-specific positive affine scaling, (iii) percentile logistic, (iv) rank/quantile mapping, and (v) logistic plus the same simple task-balanced sampler. Log per-task group reward variance, zero-variance frequency, normalized advantage distribution, entropy and patient/video-level outcomes. A replay-only mathematical/gradient test is the cheap first step. Primary endpoint: source-held-out task-macro score with video as independent unit; external clinician scores for captioning. **GO** for nonlinear calibration only if it beats affine/rank/sampling controls across seeds; **REFORMULATE** if simpler clipping or sampling suffices; **KILL** the specific scale-mismatch explanation if affine scaling is mathematically inert in the actual implementation.

## Independent novelty and MRI transfer

MedGRPO already demonstrates medical multi-task GRPO with normalized heterogeneous rewards; “balanced medical RL” is not a new MRI method by itself. The remaining space is scientifically valid **MRI-specific** task verification and preventing negative transfer among quantitative volume, spatial and longitudinal tasks. Its surgical-video judge cannot be transplanted as ground truth for MRI disease progression. A genuine transfer would need patient-linked 3D scans, independent spatial/volumetric references, acquisition-aware change thresholds and a balanced-versus-single-task comparison that includes this paper's logistic normalization as a strong baseline. Improvement only over raw unnormalized GRPO would reproduce existing work.

## Missing evidence and uncertainties

Training-code parity, patient/video leakage audit, multi-seed stability, and independent clinician-endpoint replication are **NOT_VERIFIED**. The paper reports neither native 3D MRI nor longitudinal patient MRI.
