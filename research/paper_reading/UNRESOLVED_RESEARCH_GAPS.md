# Test image dependence before MRI RL

**Q3 — What remains missing?** The literature has separate pieces of longitudinal T1 verification, MRI frame grounding, medical multi-task RL and multi-sequence follow-up evaluation. It has not, in the inspected primary sources, cleanly shown **patient-independent paired-image change caused by using both registered volumes** and then isolated a balanced-RL benefit over simple controls. This is a scoped finding from 13 papers, **not an exhaustive priority claim**. [LoV3D](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.12071v2) supplies prior FreeSurfer labels and clinical scores; [BrReMark](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25894v1) selects an oracle lesion-rich slice; [SGMRI-VQA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.15808v1) scores frame boxes; [NeuroQA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.20525v1) contains repeated-scan items but no learned RL method; [BrainTRACE](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.06571v1) evaluates repeat scans without providing an RL intervention.

## The real-data dependency is the present bottleneck

The project's [Dallas pilot](../../results/EXECUTION_STATUS.md) verified eight actual NIfTI files representing two people, two visits and T1/FLAIR per visit, with one FLAIR pair changing through-plane spacing from 6 to 2.5 mm. That is **data access and rendering feasibility**, not an independent change label, lesion mask, case report, meaningful patient sample or inference result. The first paired-volume study needs a larger rights-cleared cohort with subject/study linkage, repeat visit dates, acquisition consistency, independent measurement or adjudicated change, and sealed held-out patients. If only cross-sectional FreeSurfer spreadsheets are available, a numeric consistency task can be tested, but claims of biological progression or pathology should be withheld. Use T1 pairs with comparable acquisition first; preserve FLAIR as a protocol-shift stress test only after a valid target is defined.

BrainTRACE must remain **sealed evaluation-only** because its [paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.06571v1), [DUA notice](https://github.com/lanqz7766/BrainTRACE/blob/main/DUA_NOTICE.md) and changing [scoring repository](https://github.com/lanqz7766/BrainTRACE/blob/main/scoring/README.md) do not license tuning on hidden cases. Its two dataset accounts and `gpt-4o` versus current `gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18` default require pinned revisions and judge settings before exact reproduction ([source reconciliation](SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md)).
