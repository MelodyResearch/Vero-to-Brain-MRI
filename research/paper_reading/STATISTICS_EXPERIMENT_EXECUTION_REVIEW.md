## Prespecified next experiment: verifier challenge on real observations

### Estimands and units

The unit of sampling and uncertainty is **one subject**, with both visits, modalities, views, perturbations and generated questions clustered within that subject. Create one prespecified challenge bundle per eligible subject; if multiple regions are scored, first aggregate within subject, then estimate across subjects. Pair comparisons use the *same* subject/region and input budget for each verifier arm. Do not count every altered slice or every possible wrong-person pairing as a new independent patient. In the current 12-person set, prefer a complete descriptive census of eligible cases over inferential language.

Separate two estimands:

1. **Provenance/geometry safety:** among challenges that should be rejected or marked indeterminate, the patient-mean **false acceptance rate**. A wrong person, reversed chronology, wrong sequence, inconsistent orientation/affine, failed registration or unsupported fabricated number has a known *engineering* verdict, provided the transformation is recorded and the original scan is valid. Report rejection, false acceptance and abstention as three exhaustive outcomes. Do not silently count abstention as a correct rejection.
2. **Image-supported interval change:** among independently adjudicated, repeatability-qualified positive or negative change cases, patient-mean acceptance of supported claims and rejection of unsupported ones, with **coverage** over *all* eligible patients. There are currently **zero verified biological-change cases** in the audit; mark this estimand `NOT_ESTIMABLE` until a reference independent of the training verifier exists.

For both, show numerator/denominator by challenge type, patient count, missingness and failure reasons. Conditional accuracy among non-abstentions must always accompany coverage and an unconditional score that treats abstention as failure to decide. To avoid an apparently perfect but uninformative verifier, include a true accepted **provenance-valid pair** as an engineering positive; do not equate that with a biologically supported positive change.

### Challenge generation and controls

Freeze challenge definitions, thresholds and seed before seeing outputs. For each of the 12 T1 subjects, use its true chronological pair, reversed order and one deterministically sampled wrong-person prior from the same split; match acquisition as closely as possible to avoid a trivial scanner cue. Sequence mismatch is possible only where a real FLAIR exists and should be reported with that smaller denominator, not extended synthetically to 12 patients. Construct and record left/right orientation and affine errors in **physical coordinates**; distinguish a header-only contradiction (deterministic metadata detection) from a consistently transformed image+header (requires anatomical evidence). For misregistration, prespecify a range of physical shifts/rotations and QC thresholds independent of the test outcomes. For protocol stress, use actual FLAIR spacing discordance and real T1 sidecar differences, but do not label an acquisition difference as tissue change. Fabricated measurement challenges should cite independently measured values or use deliberately impossible claims; a FreeSurfer sign alone cannot determine biological falsehood.

Compare three blinded components on the identical bundle: **(A)** simple ID/date/sequence/affine rules, **(B)** image registration or deterministic quantitative measurement with the same allowed images, **(C)** proposed combined verifier. Add no-image/metadata-only, header-only, intensity-summary and current-image-only controls. An ID-based rejection of wrong-person scans proves provenance checking, not visual reasoning. A correct rejection of deliberately broken geometry proves geometry checking, not change detection. Tests must include actual MRI-linked records and controlled transforms; synthetic-only fixtures establish implementation correctness but no real-data performance.

For an illustrative finite-sample limit, even **zero** false acceptances in 12 independent patient bundles has a one-sided exact 95% upper bound of about **22.1%** (`1-0.05^(1/12)`); two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson upper bound is about **26.5%**. For the ten new patients alone the corresponding two-sided bound is about **30.9%**. These bounds are even less generalizable under the age-targeted sample and correlated acquisition conditions. Therefore a 0/12 count is a useful safety diagnostic, not validation of a low error rate. Additional challenge permutations do not increase the effective patient denominator.

### Provisional GO / KILL decisions

- **GO, engineering:** all eligible real pairs pass integrity checks; provenance and geometry rules reject correctly specified violations, with no accidental acceptance in the 12-subject challenge census; valid pairs remain evaluable. This permits a larger, sealed challenge cohort. It does **not** authorize a clinical or AI reward claim.
- **REFORMULATE:** deterministic ID/affine/registration rules match the combined verifier, or most cases abstain. Keep the simple rules as a strong baseline and seek a visual task with independent truth.
- **KILL the current paired-change reward:** any wrong-person or reversed-time evidence earns a *supported-change* reward; any unsupported measured claim earns reward through a target leaked in metadata; or the only positive labels are noisy FreeSurfer differences inside unknown repeatability limits. Repair and rerun on new held-out patients before RL.
- **GO, biological evidence (future):** predeclare a target region, reference/adjudication pipeline and repeatability coefficient. If same-method test–retest within-subject SD is `σ_w`, an absolute two-visit 95% repeatability bound is approximately `1.96 × √2 × σ_w` under stable unbiased errors. This requires *separate repeated scans* and protocol-specific validation; ordinary wave1→wave2 change cannot estimate its own noise floor. Inside the bound, output `INDETERMINATE`, and count those patients in coverage denominator. Include acquisition-shift strata and blinded expert QC. The evaluation reference must not be a direct reuse of the reward-generating algorithm.

## Frozen-model endpoint and fair analysis contract

If legitimate Vero/base inference becomes possible, freeze a **subject-disjoint** training/calibration/evaluation partition before tuning prompts, slices, thresholds or render settings. With 12 patients, reserve them for engineering diagnostics; do not present a tiny held-out subset as an efficacy estimate. Keep all visits, modalities, renderings and anatomical regions from a patient together. Check aliases and source overlap with model pretraining and other datasets where possible; mark unresolved overlap `UNVERIFIED`.

A defensible first endpoint is the within-subject change in **independently adjudicated, image-supported direction accuracy** for correct pair versus current-only, scored on the same subjects and claims. Prespecify a minimum meaningful margin `δ` before opening held-out outcomes; choose it from clinical measurement utility and attainable sample size, not from the observed difference. Report per-patient paired differences with a patient-cluster interval, plus coverage and false unsupported-claim rate. When a model is stochastic, run paired inference seeds/decoding replicates *within patient* but do not treat them as new patients. Training seeds form a second level of variation: report separate per-seed patient effects and their dispersion rather than bootstrap only patients from one lucky trained checkpoint. Confidence intervals from `paired_patient_difference()` account for patient clustering in its row averaging but neither training-seed variation, site shift nor selection bias; its percentile interval on 12 age-targeted patients should be descriptive only.

A complete frozen-model ladder is no-image/metadata-only, current-only, correct pair, reversed pair, wrong-person prior, wrong-sequence prior where available, and same-input deterministic measurement. Keep model family, checkpoint revision, image representation, prompt instructions, output grammar, decoding and actual image/token budgets matched between arms; report deviations and parser failures. The evaluator must be separate from any target-forming FreeSurfer or reward route. If correct-pair performance does not exceed current-only and metadata-only by `δ`, or remains unchanged on reversed/wrong-person priors, **KILL the claim that the prior image contributes visual interval evidence** for that task. This is a task-specific falsifier, not proof that all longitudinal MRI modeling is futile.

## RL mechanism diagnostics before training

Saved rollout groups must be generated from legitimate eligible tasks or explicitly marked engineering fixtures. Replay the **same group** through raw reward, positive affine maps, MedGRPO-style nonlinear calibration, tied/untied handling and zero-variance filtering. Log per task and arm: group count, unique patients, reward distributions, zero-variance fraction, informative-group fraction, normalized-advantage quantiles, effective optimizer groups, rejected/abstained groups, actual sampled categories after filtering, visual-token totals and wall time. Under per-group standardization `A_i=(r_i-r̄)/(s_r+ε)`, positive affine `r'_i=a r_i+b` with `a>0` gives the same advantages as `ε→0`; report implementation deviations rather than attributing a gain to raw scale. Nonlinear calibration cannot separate an all-tied group. `balanced_sample()` samples with replacement, so log repeated exposure to the same patient and prohibit treating 100 repeated rollouts as 100 patients.

A future multi-task effect needs at least two scientifically valid distinct tasks, matched starting checkpoint, data, reward validity and actual budget. Estimate per-task and patient-macro paired gains, worst-task change and adverse regressions. Compare same-data SFT, task-only RL, proportional and uniform mixtures under raw and nonlinear reward, plus ratio-preserving post-filter sampling and a faithful dynamic task-weight prior where feasible ([MT-GRPO, ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ramesh26b.html)). Hold training seeds and subject splits independent; do not select a favorable arm on the final test. **KILL a distinct balance-method claim** if its gain disappears under calibrated proportional/active-group controls, equal effective updates and patient-level intervals, or if it harms an independent anatomical endpoint. The 12-subject Dallas expansion cannot supply a credible estimate of this effect.

## Significance and next action

**Scientific:** current evidence establishes authentic longitudinal image access and stable basic T1 voxel geometry in the new subset, not a new finding about atrophy, lesions or diagnosis. **AI/ML:** no visual-dependence or transfer effect has been observed; simple provenance rules are a mandatory comparator. **Experimental:** the age-targeted expansion and paired-file integrity reduce access uncertainty; registration, QC, labels, scanner confounding, reference independence and sample size remain. The next highest-information action is a preregistered, patient-bundled **real-image verifier challenge** that reports what deterministic rules already solve, followed by independent repeatability-qualified change labeling only if a suitable cohort/reference can be obtained.
