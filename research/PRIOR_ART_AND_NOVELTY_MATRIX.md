# Independent prior-art audit: Vero transfer to longitudinal brain MRI

Checked 2026-10-15. Primary papers and official repository pages only. This note is evidence for the project's novelty matrix, not evidence that any MRI model has been trained here.

## Vero: what actually transfers

- **Version:** arXiv:2604.04917v3 (18 June 2026). The official repository's June 17 second release adds Qwen3.5-9B checkpoints and Vero-1.6M / 2.5M-unfiltered data. Paper experiments generally use Vero-600K unless specified. Sources: [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917), [official repository](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero), [data documentation](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero/blob/main/docs/DATA.md).
- **Curation and balance:** 600K examples from 59 datasets, uniformly 100K examples in six *general visual* categories. Over 250 candidate datasets; heuristic screening, ~50-example manual review per dataset, Qwen3-VL-235B question filtering and Qwen3-235B answer canonicalization. The six categories are Chart/OCR, STEM, Spatial/Action, Knowledge/Recognition, Grounding/Counting/Search, Captioning/Instruction Following. Medical image understanding appears within STEM; this is not a longitudinal MRI corpus. Uniform category sampling beat tested difficulty-, image-area-, and reasoning-length-weighted mixtures by overall general visual score (+5.8 points over base for uniform). See [paper sections 3.1–3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917) and [data documentation](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero/blob/main/docs/DATA.md).
- **Optimization:** single-stage online GSPO from existing checkpoints, group-normalized advantages, eight rollouts per prompt in current training documentation, sequence-level importance ratios, asymmetric clipping, no KL term, and a 2,048-token overlong penalty ramp. Paper reward is `0.8 accuracy + 0.2 format + overlong`; format expects nonempty `<think>` and `<answer>` tags and usually one boxed final answer for discrete types. Current documentation states batch 256, LR 1e-6, 4,000 steps, 8×80GB A100/H100 hardware. See [paper section 3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917) and [training guide](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero/blob/main/docs/TRAINING.md).
- **Reward routing:** ten accuracy routes: string, multiple choice, numeric with optional tolerance, list, ordering, web action, grounding, clicking, instruction following, LLM judge. Grounding uses Hungarian box matching and IoU/F1 at threshold 0.5; clicking uses point in box. Ordering gives discounted partial reward for correct set/wrong order. Open-ended reward uses an LLM judge and explicit anti-hacking rules. These are inherited/adapted verifier operations, not MRI-specific inventions. See [paper reward section](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917), [current reward stack guide](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero/blob/main/docs/TRAINING.md).
- **Evidence and limits:** the paper trains 100K-example single-category runs and a compute-matched 100K mixed run, plus 600K full mixture, on Qwen2.5-VL and Qwen3-VL. Single-category runs often reduce out-of-category grounding and captioning scores; mixed runs yield positive category gains (+0.3 to +4.2 Qwen2.5; +1.9 to +5.2 Qwen3). This demonstrates transfer patterns for the authors' general visual taxonomy, two base families, and selected evaluation suite. It **does not** demonstrate negative transfer, balance benefit, or optimum taxonomy in longitudinal MRI. The paper itself says its taxonomy is not proven optimal, omits video/multi-turn data, and treats CoT behavior analysis as descriptive rather than causal. See [sections 5, 8](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917).
- **Open-ended retention and reward hacking:** answer-only RL collapsed Captioning/IF from 64.8 to 26.8 on the reported Qwen2.5-VL ablation; prompt/format guidance only partially recovered it; adding the captioning/IF category and judge reward reached 70.6. Authors observed judge-directed self-evaluations and fabricated measurements and added judge automatic-failure rules. These findings motivate a held-out clinical explanation retention test and independent judge validation; merely having an LLM judge does not establish clinical faithfulness. See [section 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917).
- **Implementation drift caution:** paper text names a Qwen3-32B judge; current repository training guide defaults `VLLM_JUDGE_MODEL_PATH` to Qwen3.5-27B. Pin the actual repository commit and judge checkpoint before comparing to paper results. Current `main` was browsed, but **a Git commit SHA could not be verified** in this environment (`git ls-remote` DNS blocked; GitHub web page did not expose a SHA). Do not claim code reproduction at an unspecified revision. Sources: [paper reward section](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917), [training guide](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero/blob/main/docs/TRAINING.md).

## Closest prior art and boundaries

| Work and version | What is already done | Distinction / caution for proposed project |
|---|---|---|
| [Med-R1, arXiv:2503.13939](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13939) | GRPO medical VQA across eight modalities including MRI; evaluates five question types (modality, anatomy, disease, grading, biological attributes) and SFT/zero-shot comparisons. It also reports that no-thinking can improve accuracy, challenging assumed benefit of verbose rationale. | Medical GRPO and question-type generalization are prior art. Its abstract-level generalization is not a controlled cross-skill *single-task versus balanced multi-task* transfer matrix on patient-disjoint longitudinal volumes. Reported gains do not establish clinical reliability. |
| [BrReMark, arXiv:2606.25894](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.25894) | Brain MRI anomaly localization, description, diagnosis; SFT then GRPO with format, 2D box localization, Lingshu-32B semantic reward; gating for wrong sequence and fabricated lesion on normal image. Includes synthetic anomalies and OOD NOVA evaluation. | MRI grounding + diagnostic RL + composite rewards already exist. Its paper identifies 3D volumetric/multimodal extension as future work; do not imply it proves longitudinal 3D transfer. Compare spatial and diagnosis metrics and normal-scan hallucination, but disclose different data and synthetic augmentation. |
| [LoV3D, arXiv:2603.12071v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12071) | A native 3D encoder reads longitudinal T1 MRI; structured region and progression labels plus diagnosis; normative age/sex-adjusted volume Z-scores and verifier; four sampled candidates form DPO pairs after staged encoder/SFT training. ADNI test uses 479 scans/258 subjects; external MIRIAD/AIBL results. | Longitudinal brain MRI + automatically verifiable anatomy + preference optimization are prior art. New claim cannot be 'first verifiable longitudinal 3D MRI reasoning.' Difference is controlled cross-task *online* RL transfer and potentially multi-sequence/spatial grounding if demonstrated. LoV3D prompt includes demographics, APOE, cognitive scores and prior FreeSurfer labels; current-scan FreeSurfer measurements are hidden targets. Compare image-only tasks separately from multimodal clinical tasks. |
| [MedVol-R1, arXiv:2605.26621](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26621) | GRPO after cold-start SFT for volumetric reasoning segmentation: VLM selects key axial slice and 2D boxes; frozen MedSAM2 propagates to 3D mask. Rewards include informative slice selection, 2D localization, cross-slice coherence. Experiments are on CT-ORG, AbdomenCT-1K, KiTS23. | Verifiable spatial evidence and 3D outcome already exist, but not longitudinal brain MRI or broad task-transfer matrix. Do not call its VLM a native 3D segmentation encoder: it reasons over multi-slice input and delegates mask propagation. |
| [MedGRPO, arXiv:2512.06581](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06581) | Multi-task medical video RL on heterogeneous datasets; per dataset-task sigmoid reward normalization centered on training-set SFT median and scaled by IQR; medical LLM judge. Its ablation without normalization collapses many tasks. | Reward-scale balancing and medical multi-task RL are prior art. A proposed MRI normalization must be a faithful baseline to MedGRPO before claiming a new mechanism. Caution: metrics and collapse evidence are medical videos, not MRI; their study also changes judge and dataset composition across some variants. |

## Falsifiable novelty position

The scientifically defensible near-term question is **whether MRI task interference exists and whether balanced online RL mitigates it under subject-disjoint, matched-compute controls**. This would be a domain-specific empirical contribution if demonstrated. A new *method* requires a nonredundant mechanism or a new validated capability beyond combining known GSPO/GRPO, routed rewards, median normalization, 3D rendering, and MRI data. Minimum test: fixed starting checkpoint; eligible real MRI volumes; separate grounding/anatomy/longitudinal task RL runs; proportional and uniform mixtures; MedGRPO-style normalization; same total prompts, rollouts, token budget, and evaluation representation; held-out patient-level cross-task matrix with uncertainty and open-ended retention. Pre-register kill condition: no reproducible negative transfer, or balanced mixture fails to outperform proportional and SFT baselines within uncertainty. BrainTRACE cannot substitute for training data or hyperparameter selection if evaluation-only.
