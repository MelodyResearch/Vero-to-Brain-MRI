# Independent longitudinal MRI reference-source audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-15. **Scope:** official dataset custodians and primary challenge publication. This is a source/access audit; no new dataset was downloaded, inspected at voxel level, or cleared for this project's training. “Independent” below means independent of the Dallas image/FreeSurfer workbook lineage, **not** a biological ground truth for atrophy.

## Decision

**Best next eligible source: MIRIAD, for a scan–rescan noise-floor experiment.** UCL's [official MIRIAD page](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/brain-sciences/ion/research/research-centres/dementia-research-centre/research-clinical-trials/minimal-interval-resonance-imaging-alzheimers-disease-miriad) documents 69 people (46 Alzheimer disease, 23 controls), 708 volumetric T1 scans, repeated visits from two weeks to two years, and two back-to-back scans at weeks 0, 6, and 38. It reports the same scanner and radiographer throughout. That design directly tests whether the proposed longitudinal MRI reward/answer changes under a same-day re-acquisition when appreciable biological atrophy is not expected. Its clinical MMSE by visit is separately assessed, but MMSE and screening CDR are **not** anatomical-change truth. The primary [MIRIAD atrophy challenge analysis](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1473090/) explicitly describes absence of ground-truth atrophy and evaluates repeatability, symmetry, transitivity and cohort discrimination instead. A method passing these checks can still be anatomically wrong.

**Access state:** UCL describes public research access **after XNAT registration and agreement**. Its page says raw scans can be selected from the MIRIAD project with “Download Images”; ClinicalAssessment and MR Sessions tables can be exported after login. Registration accepts a no-reidentification condition, forbids redistribution without prior agreement, requires acknowledgement/citation, and requests a copy of an accepted manuscript. I did not register, assent, or download. The linked [XNAT endpoint](https://miriad.drc.ion.ucl.ac.uk/atrophychallenge) timed out during this audit, so live portal availability and file inventory are **UNVERIFIED**. This is an explicit execution gate, not evidence that data are absent.

**Executable access path once the authorized user has working registration:**

1. Open UCL's [official MIRIAD instructions](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/brain-sciences/ion/research/research-centres/dementia-research-centre/research-clinical-trials/minimal-interval-resonance-imaging-alzheimers-disease-miriad); register in the linked XNAT project and accept its terms directly. If the endpoint still fails, contact the dataset custodian through that page before treating any mirror as authorized.
2. In XNAT, export the **MR Sessions** and **ClinicalAssessment** tables and choose a bounded sample with both back-to-back T1 scans at one visit plus a later T1. Download only those authorized images into ignored local storage; do not put IDs, voxels, or clinical rows in Git. Record source revision/export date and local hashes in a private receipt.
3. Verify subject–visit–scan keys, acquisition times and image geometry. Use the **person** as the unit for held-out inference; same-day scans are replicate measurements, not independent subjects. Reserve whole people for holdout before threshold or prompt choice.
4. Prespecify a same-day null: the longitudinal change answer should remain below a calibrated noise threshold for back-to-back acquisitions; evaluate signed change, absolute change, confidence intervals clustered by person, and symmetry under visit-order reversal. Then test the longer intervals separately. Derive thresholds only from training people and compare with image-only, metadata-only, and an established registration/atrophy pipeline. A signal that appears equally on same-day rescans fails the proposed longitudinal reward gate.

## Immediate research use and KILL gate

The first use is **external falsification of a proposed change verifier**: a thresholded or graded reward for T1 longitudinal anatomical change should remain stable across MIRIAD same-day repeats and should preserve direction under scan-order reversal on longer intervals. This requires two controls: a pure imaging registration/volumetry baseline and a metadata-only model. If the proposed reward treats same-day repeat scans as disease change at a rate comparable to its long-interval detections, or merely reproduces acquisition/visit metadata, **KILL that reward definition** before any RL transfer. Passing this gate would establish measurement stability under one acquisition setting; it would not establish patient-level clinical validity or algorithmic novelty. An independent expert-reviewed anatomical-change reference remains **UNVERIFIED** in all five candidate releases audited here.

## Search record and limits

Primary-source search on 2026-10-15 covered official UCL MIRIAD and XNAT access text, official OASIS/Washington University pages and access FAQ, NITRC and Kennedy Krieger Kirby21 pages, and the MIRIAD challenge primary publication. Search terms included “MIRIAD longitudinal scan rescan access manual segmentation,” “OASIS-2 longitudinal MRI download CDR,” “OASIS-3 FreeSurfer DUA,” and “Kirby21 scan rescan registration.” Some OASIS and XNAT pages intermittently returned HTTP errors/timeouts through this audit interface, so live file inventories and signed-in access were not tested. No protected or registration-gated data were retrieved.
