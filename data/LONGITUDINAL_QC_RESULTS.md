**Execution date:** 2026-10-15. **Status:** `TESTED_CPU` for bounded image registration and engineering QC; `NOT_RUN` for medical-image model inference or RL training; **no biological change label validated**. The input is the **20-subject, 40-T1-volume** local Dallas set verified in [DALLAS_EXPANSION_AUDIT.md](DALLAS_EXPANSION_AUDIT.md): two original pilot pairs, ten first-expansion pairs, and eight second-expansion pairs. All image data, exact subject IDs, transformations, and visual contact sheets remain under ignored `local_data/`.

## Procedure and execution evidence

`src/mri_rl/longitudinal_qc.py` loads each same-person wave1 and wave2 T1 NIfTI, rejects non-3D or singular/nonfinite physical affines, checks voxel spacing, and performs a 6-degree-of-freedom rigid alignment of wave2 to wave1 in **world millimetres**. It first scales positive T1 intensities robustly, estimates a centre-of-mass start, and optimizes 24-bin mutual information on 4-mm resampled grids with DIPY 1.12.1. Translation and rotation are estimated; scaling, shearing, deformable warping, skull stripping, segmentation, and change inference are excluded. A distinct 3-mm resampling grid is used for the before/after engineering comparison. The no-registration control samples the untransformed wave2 in wave1 physical space. The reported foreground Dice uses a common relative-intensity threshold (0.05 after 99.5th-percentile scaling); Pearson intensity correlation is measured on overlapping foreground. These are *image-similarity* proxies, not anatomical accuracy or clinical endpoints.

The first execution on the pilot and first-expansion receipts processed **12/12 T1 pairs** in **5.20 seconds** on the local CPU. The expanded execution called `run_local_audit(...)` on all three ignored private receipts and processed **20/20 T1 pairs** in **9.91 seconds**. A synthetic known-translation test and malformed-affine test passed (`PYTHONPATH=src pytest -q tests/test_longitudinal_qc.py`: **2 passed**). The private result is `local_data/longitudinal_t1_registration_private.json`; contact sheets are `local_data/longitudinal_registration_qc_montage.png`, `local_data/all_pairs_three_plane_qc_montage.png`, `local_data/held_pair_three_plane_qc.png`, and `local_data/second_batch_three_plane_qc_montage.png`. All 20 input pairs have native 1-mm isotropic voxel spacing and 256×256×160 native array shape; the affines differ across visits and must be used. Estimated rigid rotation angles span **1.95°–9.56°** and translation magnitudes **2.84–24.56 mm**. The provisional numerical flag requires final Dice ≥0.75 and Pearson ≥0.50, with neither metric falling by more than 0.01; **20/20 pass this numerical flag** (first 12: 12/12; new 8: 8/8). This flag checks a gross failure mode; it is not a gold-standard registration verdict. We visually screened midpoint sagittal, coronal, and axial overlays for all 20 pairs in private contact sheets. The originally flagged pair has a conspicuous peripheral/field-of-view discontinuity visible in sagittal and axial views before and after registration. Brain contours show no obvious gross shift in those displayed planes, but the persistent nonbrain signal can inflate or destabilize full-volume similarity and makes this pair **ineligible for downstream image-evaluation tasks pending full manual QC or a validated brain mask**. The new eight pairs show no obvious gross intracranial displacement in their displayed three-plane slices; some show peripheral or lower-face coverage differences. The original other eleven were similar at the selected planes. This screen cannot exclude localized misregistration elsewhere in the 3D volumes. The private receipt marks **one `HELD_ARTIFACT` and nineteen `NOT_CERTIFIED`**; **all twenty are `image_evaluation_eligible: false` pending expert or validated automated QC**. A 3-mm evaluation grid differs from the 4-mm fitting grid, but it is still resampled from the same images; the metrics are not independent evidence of true anatomy correspondence. No expert image-quality grade or uncertainty estimate was obtained.

## FreeSurfer-derived measurement gate

The public `derivatives/brainsummary/Template_Structural_MRI.xlsx` exists locally and opens. It has **15 sheets** (cortical thickness, grey-matter volume, surface area, subcortical volume, and global variables for waves 1–3), with 464 apparent data rows per wave in most sheets. Header columns include `S#`, `AIRC_ID`, `Wave`, `HasData`, and region summaries. The sampled `AIRC_ID` values use a different identifier system from the BIDS `sub-*` paths. An authoritative crosswalk from BIDS subject and exact T1 scan to workbook row, FreeSurfer processing version/logs, segmentation QC, and paired measurement repeatability was **not verified** in the accessible local evidence. The workbook therefore supplies *available numbers*, not presently defensible per-image ground truth or a change reward. Even with a crosswalk, a raw regional sign across approximately four years cannot be called atrophy/progression without test–retest or scanner/site change thresholds, independent review, and exclusion of segmentation failures.

## Decision for the next experiment

**GO:** reproducible physical-space pairing, gross rigid-alignment QC, and controlled verifier challenges that mutate identity, visit order, affine, and image evidence. **HOLD:** image-evaluation eligibility for the artifact pair, region-specific change reward, clinical progression statements, image-grounded longitudinal VLM accuracy, and any Vero/MedGRPO/MT-GRPO benefit claim. The next gate is to obtain the exact image-to-derivative crosswalk and FreeSurfer QC/provenance, complete full-volume/three-plane expert or validated automated image QC (especially the flagged artifact), measure scan–rescan or equivalent repeatability, and only then define resolvable change with an abstention band. Expand to a patient-disjoint cohort before estimating model performance. Rigid registration alone does not solve those gates.
