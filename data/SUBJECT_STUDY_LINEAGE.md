# Subject and study lineage

The Dallas feasibility pilot verified **2 distinct subjects, 4 subject-wave sessions, and 8 authentic 3D NIfTI volumes**: one T1 MPRAGE and one FLAIR at each of waves 1 and 2. The two T1 pairs and two FLAIR pairs have matching BIDS subject IDs, distinct file hashes, positive elapsed MRI intervals from `participants.tsv`, valid sidecars, finite voxels and affines, and anatomical image renders. Every local payload byte count and SHA-256 matches the corresponding Git-annex key at pinned metadata commit `077d0a4e4f32b7c18e3628893a96708ef24e1178` (release 1.3.0). Exact subject-to-volume paths, hashes, geometry, and evidence images are in ignored `local_data/ds004856_evidence/private_receipt.json`.

This is an ingestion and rendering result. No clinical label, voxel mask, region box, model response, patient-independent test cohort, or biological change was verified. One FLAIR pair differs in through-plane spacing (6 mm versus 2.5 mm). The renderer preserves physical aspect in displayed coronal and sagittal views, while the protocol difference remains a scientific confound. The paired FreeSurfer 5.3 summary workbook is available locally, but its cross-sectional measurements are not independent diagnoses or a validated longitudinal change reference.

Before an experiment, split subjects first and keep all waves, modalities, derivatives and prompts of each subject together. Record run selection and image QC, use a larger patient-separated cohort, predeclare measurement repeatability thresholds, and compare against no-image and metadata controls. A two-subject sample cannot support a patient-level uncertainty interval or transfer claim.
