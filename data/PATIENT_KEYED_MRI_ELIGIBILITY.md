# Patient-keyed real MRI eligibility for the Vero transfer

**Audit date:** 2026-10-15. **Scope:** patient linkage, public terms, actual MRI/mask bytes, and a small held-out engineering cohort. No Qwen3-VL or Vero inference or training result is claimed here.

## Decision

**OpenNeuro `ds007045` v2.0.1 is the first locally verified multi-category brain MRI source with explicit patient keys.** Its official [dataset release](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds007045/versions/2.0.1), [public BIDS metadata repository](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds007045), and pinned `dataset_description.json` at metadata commit `283007b96977b5b3285bd22cd67b7d97114b1eed` state CC0 and DOI `10.18112/openneuro.ds007045.v2.0.1`. The current metadata has 363 unique `participants.tsv` participant IDs and a matching segmentation-mask file under each subject folder. Subject folder keys provide a documented *within-source* patient unit. The [source README](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds007045/blob/main/README.txt) says masks were initialized by a SegResNet model and subsequently reviewed/corrected by neuroradiologists. Accordingly they are **expert-reviewed image-derived references, not independent wholly hand-traced masks or biological ground truth**. The [primary data paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07953-2) describes an earlier 337-patient v1.0.1 release; do not substitute its cohort count or QC claims for all 363 v2.0.1 cases without version reconciliation.

## Actual 40-patient acquisition and validation

Using **metadata only**, before inspecting the new masks, we selected one bounded cohort across all eight site prefixes: BA 3, BO 7, FO 5, JS 5, KA 7, ME 4, NSK 5, TO 4. Within each site, we selected the lexicographically earliest subjects with a processed contrast-enhanced T1 MRI, processed FLAIR MRI and matching tumor mask in the pinned Git-annex metadata. One already-inspected KA pilot subject was retained instead of the seventh earliest KA subject. This is a deterministic convenience sample, **not** a representative site sample. We preassigned **32 training patients and eight held-out patients**, one held out per site, choosing the lexicographically latest selected patient at each site *excluding all eight initially inspected pilot patients*. Exact pseudonymous IDs and source paths are only in ignored `local_data/ds007045_bounded/private_receipt_40.json`.

From the official public OpenNeuro S3 bucket, we downloaded **120 files**: 40 contrast-enhanced T1 volumes, 40 FLAIR volumes and 40 tumor masks, totaling **512,484,347 bytes (about 489 MiB)**. Every file's byte length and SHA-256 matched its pinned Git-annex key. For all 40 patients, both MRI modalities and the mask decode as finite nonempty 182 × 218 × 182 3D arrays; affines match within each trio, spacing is 1 mm isotropic, and mask values are the expected {0,1,2,3}. The source raw BIDS sidecars `_run-02_T1w.json` and `_FLAIR.json` exist for every selected patient and establish sequence identity independently of tumor masks; the README maps `run-02_T1w` to contrast-enhanced T1. Sidecars and identifiers are not model inputs or Git artifacts. The checked mask labels are 0 background, 1 necrotic core, 2 non-enhancing/edematous tumor and 3 contrast-enhancing tumor as specified by the [data paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07953-2), **different from MSD Task01's remapped labels**.

These checks establish real image availability, byte integrity, within-patient registration of the processed derivatives, and label syntax for the bounded sample. They do **not** establish clinical accuracy of each mask, lack of prior model pretraining exposure, cross-dataset patient nonoverlap, or a statistically precise estimate from eight held-out patients. Registered derivatives may share preprocessing artifacts that a model can exploit.

## Image-only question pack and reference distribution

The ignored `local_data/ds007045_bounded/visual_prompts/` holds **360 PNGs**, a `prompts_image_only.jsonl` with **543** questions, a separate `gold_mask_derived_PRIVATE.jsonl`, and a private subject/renderer manifest. The prompt file has no source subject IDs. Rendering uses axial `z=45,91,136`, fixed geometric quartiles from the 182-slice shape **before checking those slices' masks**. Each displayed MRI channel is normalized using only that patient's image intensities, oriented consistently and doubled in display resolution. Single-sequence questions show an unlabelled FLAIR or contrast-enhanced T1 image. Tumor questions show the two MRI panels with sequence names but no mask or answer overlay. Gold answers come from hidden mask voxels on the corresponding slice. The loader must pass only image pixels and question text to a model.

| Category | Reference source | Train prompts | Held-out prompts | Training-majority answer and held-out accuracy |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Sequence recognition | BIDS acquisition identity, independent of tumor mask | 192 | 48 | Two classes tied in train; either fixed class gives **24/48 = 50.0%**. |
| Any-tumor presence | Reviewed tumor mask on fixed slice | 96 | 24 | Train majority yes (52/96); on held-out **11/24 = 45.8%**. |
| Contrast-enhancing presence | Mask class 3 on fixed slice | 96 | 24 | Train majority no (61/96); on held-out **16/24 = 66.7%**. |
| 3 × 3 tumor-centroid grid | Mask-positive voxels on fixed slice; emitted only if tumor present | 52 | 11 | Train majority left-middle (18/52); on held-out **2/11 = 18.2%**. |

There are **436 training prompts and 107 held-out prompts**, but the independent units are **32 and eight patients**, respectively. Derived tumor questions from one mask are strongly correlated. The split is valid for a patient-held-out *engineering comparison* inside ds007045; only eight test patients, one per site, limit precision. A later larger split should be frozen before model tuning and evaluated with patient-clustered confidence intervals. No model has been run on this pack as part of this data audit.

## Crosswalk findings for earlier MSD sources

- **MSD Task04 Hippocampus:** the official [challenge page](https://medicaldecathlon.com/) lists 394 cropped volumes, while the downloaded MONAI archive contains 390 (260 labeled training, 130 unlabeled challenge test), and the [MSD paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-30695-9) describes 195 underlying adults. The archive's `dataset.json` contains volume filenames and mask paths, **no source patient or left/right crop crosswalk**. A two-crop-per-person interpretation is plausible from the counts but remains **UNVERIFIED for individual files**. Do not split its 260 labeled volumes as though they were 260 independent patients.
- **MSD Task01 BrainTumour:** embedded `dataset.json` lists 484 labeled cases and 266 unlabeled challenge test images by anonymized `BRATS_*.nii.gz` filenames but supplies **no original BraTS patient identifier mapping**. Eight image/mask pairs are locally verified, yet those filenames alone cannot establish external patient nonoverlap with ds007045 or a patient-level split. Source [BraTS 2017 documentation](https://www.med.upenn.edu/sbia/brats2017/data.html) distinguishes expert manual annotations from older algorithm-fused labels; exact per-MSD-case provenance remains unresolved.

## Other eligible source screened

OpenNeuro `ds006248` offers 50 BIDS patient folders and 50 expert-reviewed ground-truth pituitary masks in its [official metadata repository](https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds006248). Its README says **CC BY-NC 4.0**, while its `dataset_description.json` at metadata commit `1b8ebd1f1171049c7f4258ea41933e7b8ee6c06e` says **CC0**. The terms conflict in the same official release; no voxels were downloaded in this audit and we will not treat CC0 as settled without custodian clarification. The source is also anatomically distinct from parenchymal glioblastoma and should be a separate task/source cell, not silently merged with its labels.

## Immediate use

Use the ds007045 prompt pack as the first **within-source, patient-held-out** two-category comparison for matching base Qwen3-VL and pretrained Vero checkpoints: BIDS-verified sequence identity versus mask-verified tumor questions, with the majority baselines above and image-shuffled/no-image controls. Keep the eight held-out patients out of SFT, RL reward construction, sampling decisions, threshold tuning and checkpoint selection. Then compare with the MSD Task01 and Task04 tasks as *different source domains*, without claiming cross-source patient isolation or label equivalence until lineage and ontology mapping are checked. Longitudinal Dallas remains a separate category whose reward validity is unresolved; it does not block these direct Vero transfer experiments.
