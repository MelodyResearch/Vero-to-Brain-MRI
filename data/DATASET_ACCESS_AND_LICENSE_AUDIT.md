# Dataset access and lineage audit (15 October 2026)

This is a source audit, not a local image integrity check. The execution host cannot resolve external hosts via its shell, so no restricted data, MRI images, or QA records were downloaded and no image decoding was claimed. Counts below are **publisher claims / repository display counts**, not independently enumerated local counts.

## SGMRI-VQA and fastMRI+

- The [official dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SpatialGroundingVQA/SGMRI-VQA/raw/main/README.md) reports 41,307 QA pairs total, with brain train 12,193 image QA + 3,231 volume QA = 15,424, and brain test 3,704 image QA + 1,064 volume QA = 4,768. Tasks: image detection, localization, classification, diagnosis, captioning; volume detection, localization, counting, classification, captioning. Localization answers use `<bbx>[x,y,w,h]</bbx>` and frame indices. The paper says raw HDF5 volumes were converted to grayscale PNG slices and annotations matched by volume identifier and slice index ([paper §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15808)). These are **within-volume sequential frames, not longitudinal visits**.
- The card lists `brain_train_images.tar`, `brain_test_images.tar`, and `metadata/`, but the [current top-level official file tree](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SpatialGroundingVQA/SGMRI-VQA/tree/main) shows only `brain/`, `knee/`, `.gitattributes`, `README.md`, and `croissant.json` (~51 MB). The [brain/train tree](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SpatialGroundingVQA/SGMRI-VQA/tree/main/brain/train) shows `image_qa/` and `volume_qa/`, not `metadata/`. Thus the official card and current files disagree; **official image archives are not presently verifiable in the repository file listing**. The project's [GitHub README](https://github.com/lamawmouk/SGMRI-VQA) separately states image data is not included in its code repository and provides preprocessing scripts to extract it from fastMRI+.
- A [third-party repackage](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BBexist/SGMRI-VQA-fixed) claims 1.87 GB and embeds per-slice PNG bytes in its `image_qa` Parquet, with `volume_id`, `slice_num`, `modality`, `image_path`, QA, reasoning, labels, and bounding boxes. Its `volume_qa` rows lack image bytes; `volume_metadata` lists image paths only. Its displayed counts are brain 12,193/3,704 image QA, 3,231/1,064 volume QA, and 749/247 volume-metadata rows in train/val. This third-party release is **not proof that the image redistribution complies with upstream terms**. It is also insufficient evidence of complete frame availability for each volume until every expected `slice_num`/path is checked after authorized access. The repackage's `val` corresponds numerically to official `test`; preserve the original holdout role, rather than treating it as a tuning split.
- The [NYU fastMRI access agreement](https://fastmri.med.nyu.edu/) limits the original image data to internal research/education after agreement and prohibits redistribution without prior permission. The [fastMRI+ maintainers](https://github.com/microsoft/fastmri-plus/blob/main/README.md) say source images must be obtained through fastmri.org after signing the agreement; their annotations are separate. The SGMRI dataset card also says research use is subject to the underlying fastMRI agreement. Therefore neither the HF `apache-2.0` metadata nor the third-party repackage's tag can be taken as a grant of rights to use redistributed source images. **Safe route: obtain approved fastMRI access, then reproduce/extract images locally; do not upload source images to GitHub.**
- The paper reports 996 brain volumes after filtering (with 974 knee), but experimental setup reports 670 brain training and 222 validation volumes; the repackage displays 749 train and 247 val brain metadata rows. These differences need reconciliation before asserting a unique-volume count. No public `patient_id` is evident in the displayed slice/volume schemas; `volume_id` is a study/series-level key at best. **Patient independence is unproven** from these fields, and a volume-grouped split is not automatically patient-grouped. The QA count cannot be treated as independent sample size. [Paper §3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15808); [repackage schema](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BBexist/SGMRI-VQA-fixed).
- Paper quality control describes clinician review of all 222 brain and 155 knee test volumes, but iterative review of over 300 training volumes across domains, with many corrections scripted; this does not amount to independent expert validation of every training QA. The paper also notes approx. 18% brain localization/captioning laterality errors in initial GPT-4o outputs and correction based on coordinates. Treat open-ended explanations as weaker reward targets than hidden expert boxes. [Paper §3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15808).

## BrainTRACE and MR-RATE

- [Formal BrainTRACE release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lanqz7766/BrainTRACE), version 1.0.0, is **test only**. It reports 1,778 source patients, 7,299 studies, 6,923 source rows (5,653 broadQA, 1,200 3D, 70 six-step clinical cases), and 7,273 scored instances. It contains questions, targets, rubrics, evidence pointers and cohort metadata, but **no volumes, PNGs, source radiology reports, or rendered evidence**. Do not use this release for training, reward tuning, prompt selection, threshold tuning, or model selection.
- Its displayed schema includes `item_id`, `track`, `level`, `question`, `options`, `gt_value`, `reference_answer`, `rubric_json`, `patient_id_anon`, `study_uids`, `shown_tp_labels`, `shown_relative_days`, `input_modalities`, `input_planes`, `slice_selection_rule`, `image_relpaths`, and `volume_relpath`. The `cohort/` manifests permit scoping upstream studies; identifiers are pseudonymous yet remain sensitive. Use the [official renderer](https://github.com/lanqz7766/BrainTRACE) only after legitimate MR-RATE access, keep targets/rubrics out of model prompts, and check model training subjects/studies against cohort IDs before calling results independent. [Dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lanqz7766/BrainTRACE).
- The [MR-RATE upstream HF page](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Forithmus/MR-RATE) is publicly visible but access to files requires login, agreement to share contact information, and acceptance of its terms. It allows academic/research/education, prohibits commercial use and redistribution, and forbids reidentification. This is an access gate, not authorization implied by the project's request. MR-RATE says native-space NIfTI volumes, metadata, reports, and patient-level splits are in the main 8.1 TB repository; the current public description reports 83,425 patients, 98,334 studies, 705,254 series and provides train/validation/test counts. Additional coreg, atlas, segmentation repos are separately packaged. BrainTRACE authors require use of the upstream access terms/DUA even though BrainTRACE's own metadata has CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0. [BrainTRACE access statement](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lanqz7766/BrainTRACE), [MR-RATE terms](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Forithmus/MR-RATE).

## NeuroQA

- [Official NeuroQA GitHub](https://github.com/mhabbasiit/neuroqa) reports 56,953 QA pairs / 12,977 subjects across 12 sources. Tier 1 comprises 19,262 questions / 2,467 subjects from BraTS-GLI, BraTS-MEN, CC359, HCP-YA, IXI, WMH; Tier 2 comprises 37,691 / 10,510 from ABCD, ADNI, AIBL, HCP-Aging, HCP-Development, PPMI and is generated by scripts after upstream DUA access. Public HF JSON contains only 17,303 Tier-1 items (13,598 train, 1,892 validation, 1,813 public test); the remaining 1,959 Tier-1 items are private test. Do not conflate full-paper counts with public runnable counts.
- The [HF dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mhabbasiit/NeuroQA?duplicate=true) explicitly says **QA JSON only, no images**, and `image_path`, `image_path_t2`, `image_path_prior` are `"#"` placeholders. Users must separately obtain source volumes and run the published sMRI skull-strip/MNI registration pipeline, then map `subject_id` plus `session_date` to local images. CC-BY-4.0 on QA does not license source volumes. Public Tier 1 still requires source-specific terms/access, including BraTS and HCP. The paper says partitions are subject-level with no subject in multiple splits ([paper §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20525)); verify on the raw JSON and local images before any evaluation. HF's auto-converted viewer may combine `all_public` and `per_dataset` files and inflate apparent row counts; use explicit `all_public/*.json` paths.
- The [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20525) distinguishes image-grounded items from image-informed answers that depend on FreeSurfer volumetry, clinical instruments or other nonvisual information. Diagnostic, severity and some reasoning labels should **not** be used as verifiable image-only RL targets merely because numerical answers exist. The paper includes a Longitudinal category, but whether a particular public item has actual prior scans must be checked with `session_date`/placeholder prior paths and source volumes. Quantitative measures must remain hidden targets, and model-visible evidence must support the asked category.
- Potential overlap with BrainTRACE is lower for the listed Tier-1 sources than for MR-RATE, but do not assert independence without identifier/source-lineage checks, especially if any MR-RATE-derived data are later added. NeuroQA is an additional independent-source evaluation candidate only after actual eligible images have been acquired and linked.

## Immediate contract for implementation

1. Record exact source revision/hash and agreement status. Keep restricted inputs, identifiers and source reports outside GitHub.
2. For each authorized source, enumerate unique patient → study/visit → series/volume → slice → QA. Verify every QA image path resolves and decodes; verify box coordinates are within pixel bounds and frame/slice indices resolve to the same volume. Report exact usable counts only after these checks.
3. Hold out by patient when IDs exist; otherwise say patient isolation unverified and avoid patient-independent claims. Keep SGMRI official test, BrainTRACE test and NeuroQA public/private test out of tuning.
4. For the first real SGMRI image test, use the signed fastMRI route and author's extraction code, or secure explicit written clarification for redistributed PNG use. Public JSON permits parser/schema work now, but it does not itself establish a legally usable image+QA evaluation sample.
