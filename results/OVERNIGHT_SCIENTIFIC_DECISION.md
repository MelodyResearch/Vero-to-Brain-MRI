## Root-cause adjudication

The current strongest *verified* cause of zero direct-profile updates in the **historical SFT4 arms** is that every sampled completion received zero format and task reward under the local strict gate. The [pinned reward audit](VERO_FORMAT_AND_REWARD_CONTRACT_AUDIT.md) found one concrete parser mismatch—nested boxed answers—which has a CPU-tested correction. Separately, the missing SFT system instruction caused the first SFT28 development probe to miss every strict wrapper; the matched prompt yields 54/54 valid wrappers with the same final adapter. This specific prompt defect does **not** retroactively explain the earlier SFT4 arms, which used a different checkpoint and prompt. `enable_thinking=False` did not change rendered bytes in the pinned tokenizer. Larger rollout groups cannot rescue an all-zero reward distribution unless some samples become valid or task-correct.

The strongest *independent scientific threat* is weak visual-task validity. The [image and shortcut audit](../data/MRI_VISUAL_ANSWERABILITY_AND_SHORTCUT_AUDIT.md) re-rendered all 240 single-sequence PNGs, recomputed 120 presence, 120 enhancement and 63 grid references, and found no detected slice-rotation or mask-label coding error. But some positive slices are extremely small: a tumor-positive slice has only five native mask pixels; two enhancement-positive slices have one voxel and another has two. Twenty-three of 63 grid centroids lie within 10 display pixels of a 3×3 boundary. More decisively, a train-fitted rule using **axial level alone**, without lesion pixels, obtains **19/24** tumor-presence and **20/24** enhancement labels on the historical held-out pack. The three tumor routes also share the same image and mask lineage. These observations do not prove the VLM ignores pixels, but they make raw tumor-task accuracy and apparent cross-task transfer non-identifying without level-matched counterfactuals and stronger references. This cohort contains tumors at the case level, so a slice-level `no` is not a normal-brain control.

The original eight test patients have already influenced reward and prompt choices. Preserve their predictions as historical exploratory outcomes. Internal development can diagnose a mechanism, but a future transfer/generalization claim requires a frozen protocol and genuinely untouched patient cohort. Treat patient, not question, as the unit of uncertainty; tumor-derived questions are correlated. The current one-held-out-patient-per-site design is not an unseen-site test.

The completed SFT28 sequence controls sharpen that concern. With 24 balanced internal-development sequence questions, the matched-prompt model scored 15/24 with MRI pixels, 12/24 with no image (it always said FLAIR), and made an appropriate answer change on only 6/24 same-patient/slice FLAIR↔T1 image swaps. This is a small positive pixel signal but far from robust sequence recognition; no comparable evidence yet supports the tumor routes. The 54/54 format recovery must not be confused with visual-reasoning recovery.

## Novelty and significance boundary

The method fingerprint under test is Vero's **equal-category sampling + task-routed accuracy/format rewards + open-ended retention + group-relative GSPO** on image-linked MRI tasks. GSPO, medical visual RL, MRI grounding reward, longitudinal verifiable measurement, and multi-task medical reward normalization all have [related or direct component precedents](../research/PRIOR_ART_AND_NOVELTY_MATRIX.md): [Vero](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04917v3), [Med-R1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13939), [BrReMark](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.25894), [LoV3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12071v2), and [MedGRPO](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06581v4). The scoped verdict for **complete controlled Vero-recipe transfer to patient-disjoint brain MRI with an explicit specialist-versus-mixture matrix** is **No Direct Match Found in the previously searched record**; it is not a global priority or algorithm-novelty claim. The present local port also differs substantially from pinned official CUDA veRL: two rather than eight rollouts, 256 rather than 18,432 response tokens, rank-4 language LoRA with frozen vision rather than full-model updating, different optimizer schedule, and no judge-backed open-ended retention arm. Thus any result must be labeled a bounded MLX approximation until parity is tested.

- **MRI scientific significance now:** None established. No demonstrated improvement in diagnosis, clinically meaningful localization, lesion change, or reader-supported interpretation. Tiny-reference and axial-level shortcuts specifically weaken such claims.
- **AI/ML significance now:** A reproducible all-zero local reward bottleneck and a separate post hoc task-reward update demonstration. No causal evidence for balanced sampling, GSPO transfer, negative transfer, or a new MRI-specific optimizer.
- **Experimental significance now:** Strong: the failure is decomposable into strict output contract, non-informative groups, possible visual shortcuts, and representation/optimizer limits. These are testable in a fixed order without inventing a new algorithm.

## Strongest falsifiable next experiment

**Question:** After making the format interface exact and holding initialization/precision constant, can this VLM obtain *image-dependent* reward variance on at least two independent MRI task routes?

1. **Completed:** On the four internal-development patients, the saved starting 4-bit adapter and 28-step SFT adapter were compared under the same matched system/chat template, images and greedy decoder. Strict validity was 0/54 versus 54/54, and the final adapter answered 36/54 correctly. This diagnoses format recovery; the starting adapter's task correctness outside the strict format has not been established by a prespecified semantic parser.
2. **Partly completed:** For sequence recognition, same-patient/slice alternate-sequence and no-image controls were run. Only 6/24 swaps changed the answer in the correct direction; no-image accuracy was 12/24. Next, enlarge the patient-disjoint cohort and add a blank-image control where technically valid. For a second task, construct or select prespecified, visually adjudicated tumor cases matched on axial level and lesion area; compare real, lesion-erased/altered, same-level cross-patient, and blank evidence, with expert mask provenance kept separate. The current exact-grid label should remain diagnostic until boundary tolerance/answerability is fixed.
3. Only if at least two routes show image-grounded answerability, draw a fixed train-only prompt set. Under the exact strict reward, inspect semantic-correct/format-valid cells and sequential rollout groups of 2, 4, then at most 8 with fixed decoder and token accounting. Require at least one **task-correctness-varying group**, finite nonzero task-driven gradient, and a changed/reloadable adapter before attempting specialist/proportional/uniform arms.

**GO:** Matched-prompt SFT or an explicitly declared format intervention yields valid, variable **task** rewards; real-image accuracy and evidence-directed changes exceed label-prior, axial-level, no-image, and shuffled-image controls on independent development patients; at least two heterogeneous routes pass; a task-driven optimizer step is verified. This licenses a bounded equal-start/equal-attempt GSPO comparison, with active-update matching as a separate mechanism diagnostic. **REFORMULATE:** Valid format but no image dependence calls for task/rendering/reference or visual-adapter controls, not longer format SFT. Semantic correctness without strict format calls for a transparently separate reward-interface ablation. **KILL the immediate balance-effect claim:** any apparent mixture gain disappears against same-data SFT, proportional sampling, axial-level priors, equal informative updates, or a prospective patient cohort. None of these outcomes alone kills the entire Vero-to-MRI question.

The most informative prospective contribution would be a **patient-level cross-task matrix after real, matched MRI task-reward updates**, including open-ended retention and no-image/swap controls. Only a reproducible mechanism-specific failure of that direct recipe would justify a new MRI-specific RL algorithm claim.
