## Real MRI adapter and execution

[`vero_train_adapter.py`](../src/mri_rl/vero_train_adapter.py) converted **520** private MSD Task04 image-grounded records into official veRL JSONL, yielding **416** training rows and **104** scan-disjoint engineering dev rows. Each split contains two routes equally: training 208 spatial centroid and 208 anterior/posterior comparison; dev 52 of each. Files and image paths remain under ignored `local_data/msd_task04/vero_training_adapter_v1`. Mask-derived targets are hidden from prompt images. [`vero_mri_reward.py`](../src/mri_rl/vero_mri_reward.py) scores centroid within 3 native voxels or exact dominance, then applies Vero's 0.8 accuracy/0.2 format blend. This is an MRI verifier extension passed to the **official** veRL reward manager, not a replacement trainer. The formatter and coordinate tolerance are our engineering choices, not part of released Vero.

The adapter also consumed **16** fixed-midpoint, image-only, mask-verified prompts from the eight real MSD Task01 multisequence tumor scans: eight enhancing-presence and eight tumor-centroid-grid questions. The combined private veRL files in ignored `local_data/vero_combined_training_v1` contain **536** records, with **428** train and **108** scan-disjoint engineering dev rows. Task01 contributes six training and two dev records per category; Task04 contributes 208 training and 52 dev per category. All prompts derived from one scan remain in the same split. Patient-level lineage is still unverified for both sources.

[`vero_official_launch.py`](../src/mri_rl/vero_official_launch.py) validates the source SHA and real image paths, then builds an official Hydra `gspo_qwen3vl` invocation. Its dry run succeeded, reporting 0.5/0.5 category weights and `proportional_equals_uniform=true`. An `--execute` attempt exited before training with `Official Vero training requires the requested CUDA GPUs`; local PyTorch reported **2.11.0 / `torch.cuda.is_available() == False`**. This is an actual hardware incompatibility for upstream veRL/vLLM, not a data-verifier gate. The M3 Pro MLX backend may allow a separate quantized LoRA research smoke, but would require a new implementation and must be labeled as such.

The MSD archives do not provide stable patient identifiers. Scan-disjoint engineering dev does **not** prove patient disjointness. The Task04 pair derives from one segmentation mask and has a 94.6% posterior majority class. Task01 has only eight scans; enhancing presence has a 6/8 positive majority at the fixed midpoint. Both task pairs share a source mask within each scan. These are valid task-routing and image-grounding engineering inputs, not independent clinical skills.

The combined data finally makes the **sampling mathematics** distinct: uniform category weights are 0.25 each; proportional weights are 208/428 = **0.4860** for each Task04 category and 6/428 = **0.0140** for each Task01 category. At the official batch size of 256, the pinned largest-remainder sampler would draw 64 prompts/category for uniform and approximately 124/124/4/4 for proportional. Uniform would recycle only six Task01 training scans heavily within a batch, so effective patient exposure and repeated-scan memorization must be logged; a model accuracy difference from this tiny pool would have no scientific interpretation. Both dry-run launcher arms passed schema/path checks, but neither updated weights.

CPU verification after the Metal smoke: `PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m pytest -q` → **60 passed**; `python3 -m py_compile src/mri_rl/mlx_gspo_smoke.py` and `git diff --check` passed. The two private adapter files from the successful engineering smoke have different independently checked SHA-256 hashes below.

[`mlx_gspo_smoke.py`](../src/mri_rl/mlx_gspo_smoke.py) is a separate Apple Metal feasibility path. It loads the downloaded Qwen3-VL-8B or released Vero checkpoint, prepares 4-bit rank-4 LoRA, samples on-policy responses to a real MRI image, and refuses to optimize equal-reward groups. A successful active group would use the pinned GSPO-token stop-gradient/clipping objective and save private before/after adapter hashes. Quantization, LoRA, small groups, the prompt and compressed response budget are engineering departures from the official 8-GPU Vero recipe.

### Actual Metal training feasibility attempts

Four separately declared configurations executed on the local Apple Metal device, with up to three prespecified training prompts for the selected route, two rollouts per prompt, temperature 1.0 and seed 7. Every attempt's reward, response length and advantage is in the corresponding **ignored private** `local_data/.../receipt.json`; raw rollout text and token IDs are in adjacent `raw_rollouts.json`. These were engineering diagnostics on small, lineage-limited data, not scientific experiments.

| Model / prompt configuration | Route / response cap | Groups and reward diagnostic | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base Qwen3-VL-8B, native template, original tagged prompt | Task01 enhancing presence, 64 tokens | 3 groups / 6 responses; every response hit 64 tokens and had total reward −1.0 from the scaled overlong penalty | Zero advantages; no update |
| Released Vero Qwen3-VL-8B, native template, original tagged prompt | Task04 dominance, 128 tokens | 3 groups / 6 responses; every response hit 128 tokens and had total reward −1.0 | Zero advantages; no update |
| Released Vero checkpoint, **shared base Qwen3 chat template** SHA-256 `5c72a170d2a4a1a3bc5adad2e689ae28138a9700e5b8c96c0266331e86c0acce`, separate concise tagged prompt | Task01 enhancing presence, 128 tokens | 3 groups / 6 responses across both hidden yes/no labels; responses ended in 6–10 tokens, but all had total reward 0.0 because the model emitted a boxed answer without the required `<think>...</think><answer>...</answer>` wrapper | Zero advantages; no update |
| Released Vero checkpoint, native template, original tagged prompt, separately declared longer budget | Task04 dominance, 512 tokens | 1 group / 2 responses; lengths 362 and 221. Both had task accuracy 0 and format 0; the longer response alone incurred −0.4140625 overlong penalty versus 0.0. Advantages −0.707104/0.707104. | **One rank-4 LoRA optimizer step**, gradient L2 0.244089; weight files changed |

The shared-template run isolates *reward sparsity under the official format gate*: the model's bare boxed answers receive zero accuracy because they omit the tag wrapper. This matches pinned Vero's [`format_reward`](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero/blob/025ca3c800fc95224c86323c1f7fd33dceed3090/vero-rl/vero_reward/math_verify_reward_type_boxed.py#L1027-L1062), which requires exactly one nonempty `<think>` and `<answer>` pair, and [`compute_score`](https://github.com/zlab-princeton/vero/blob/025ca3c800fc95224c86323c1f7fd33dceed3090/vero-rl/vero_reward/math_verify_reward_type_boxed.py#L1116-L1135), which **sets accuracy to zero whenever format score is zero**. A format-tolerant reward would be a declared new MRI-specific reward ablation, not the direct recipe transfer.

The fourth run establishes a genuine, independently verified **weight update**, but its advantage came entirely from the overlong penalty. It supplies no evidence of improved MRI recognition or reasoning. The private `before_adapters.safetensors` SHA-256 is `502084c8a1394fdffd765741b9b0fbfcb6720268dfe7671c20d7a67dc5f86088`; `after_adapters.safetensors` is `4f0916be597ff618328910883342ba7304c0496552001b73cb991d01b10b4c1d`. This is one optimizer step on one Task04 scan with unverified patient lineage, not a controlled base-versus-Vero comparison. Adapter reload and heldout evaluation remain unverified. The next method test must obtain task-accuracy or valid-format reward variance on patient-keyed MRI, then compare arms from identical starting weights.

The four historical Metal attempts used the now explicitly named `scaled_smoke` overlong profile, with a buffer equal to half the short local response cap. The new `direct_vero` profile uses the pinned 2,048-token buffer and 18,432-token maximum; its overlong penalty is exactly zero for all locally capped 16–512-token responses. The MRI callback's tag gate now mirrors pinned Vero's full-match and nonempty-reasoning checks, while its boxed MRI answer extraction remains task-specific. This direct profile is CPU-tested but has **not yet produced a Metal update**. Even then, the MLX actor would still differ from official Vero through 4-bit language-only LoRA, group size, category schedule and optimizer. Each single on-policy group begins with a sequence ratio of one, so the clipping branch has not been exercised by the one-step smoke.
