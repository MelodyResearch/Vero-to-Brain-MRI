## B. Loader memory footprint (source inspection of installed mlx 0.32.4 / mlx-vlm 0.7.8)
- `load_model`: `mx.load` of each shard is lazy (mmap-backed); the merged `weights` dict holds lazy handles, not copies. The model skeleton's parameters are random-initialized lazily and are never evaluated when replaced by `load_weights`. `load_model_weights(lazy=True)` does not `mx.eval`. I found **no eager duplicate of the base weights** in this path.
- Quantization (4-bit, group 64) is applied to the lazy graph in `prepare_model_for_training`; evaluating a quantized leaf reads that leaf's bf16 pages once and produces packed weight, scales and biases in one primitive. Resident result is the quantized model; the bf16 source exists only as page-cache pages of the mmap'd files, which are file-backed and reclaimable.
- Existing project settings already bound allocator slack: `mx.set_cache_limit` 256 MiB, serial leaf materialization with `gc.collect` + `clear_cache`, observation every 64 leaves.
- OBSERVED prior successful load: peak MLX 5.9776 GiB. OBSERVED failed attempt: at 960 leaves MLX active 1.94 GiB while swap grew 1.49 GiB with ~6.5 GiB reported available, i.e. growth roughly tracked newly allocated MLX bytes. **INFERRED (not causal proof):** with ~6 GiB needed resident and ~6 GiB reported available, any loader that must hold the quantized model leaves no slack, so the OS pays for the allocation with swap/compression. Whether that displaces other apps' pages is not identifiable from global telemetry.
- Therefore the evidence-based result is: **minimum resident footprint ~6 GiB for this frozen 4-bit model; no avoidable duplicate or intermediate in the loader was found; no numerically neutral reduction exists** (precision, weights, scoring, preprocessing are frozen). The 8 GiB floor is a heuristic of ~6 GiB resident + ~2 GiB slack, consistent with this. I do **not** recommend lowering it or changing the 1 GiB stage cap on this evidence. The remaining honest options are (i) wait for headroom (done by the finite launcher below, without LLM turns), or (ii) a separately reviewed, explicitly scoped change in how the stage swap cap is interpreted during loading; I am not proposing (ii) without measurements that distinguish own-allocation swap from system-wide drift (needs a per-process swap/compressed-memory metric, not currently sampled).
- Not inspected (NOT_VERIFIED): actual model-dir shard sizes/dtype (local_data listing is denied to me). Codex can supply aggregate shard bytes and `quantization` presence.
