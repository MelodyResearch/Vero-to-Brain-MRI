## 1. Source evidence and label boundary

The pinned ds007045 README at commit `283007b96977b5b3285bd22cd67b7d97114b1eed` states that this is a multicenter primary-glioblastoma cohort, and its tumor masks were generated by SegResNet following BraTS conventions and subsequently reviewed/corrected by neuroradiologists. The README identifies run01 as precontrast T1 and run02 as postcontrast T1, and describes spatially corresponding segmentation and registered, skull-stripped, N4-corrected derivatives. README SHA256: `66efa8e9ffd194b0aca9e52e9a62201db1b447f9b1f38a11ec8daec265293d96`.

This is **source-reported annotation provenance**, not a new independent expert adjudication of our 2D prompts. Individual correction histories, inter-rater agreement, scanner-specific reliability and visible answerability of each chosen frame remain unverified. A zero mask on a slice does not mean a healthy patient. Postcontrast T1 alone does not independently establish pre/post enhancement. Source mask labels and visible clinical judgments remain different endpoints.

The old 28 training patients and ten selected C2 additions were checked against source split identities. The additional patients remain outside the original 40 and reserved 14; the reserved cohort was checked by its existing exclusion identity list only, without accessing its images or outcomes. Scanner manufacturer/protocol fields are not verified in this analysis; site labels are known, but cannot substitute for scanner provenance.

## 3. Actual leave-one-site-out image baselines

Features were fixed before execution: the existing C1/C2 16×16 bilinear grayscale thumbnail divided by 255, and 16-bin full-image grayscale histogram normalized to sum 1. No feature or distance tuning occurred. For every query, neighbors from its **entire site and its patient** were excluded; squared Euclidean distance with record-ID tie breaking selected 1NN. Each sequence slice was evaluated separately, with both modalities from each patient kept in the same held-out-site fold. Fold-majority controls were fitted only on the remaining sites; level-majority uses remaining-site cases at the same slice, falling back to remaining-site majority if necessary.

Site-equal thumbnail accuracy is 65.625%,33.333%,95.833%,86.458%,92.708% in the table's row order. Histogram site-equal accuracy is 56.771%,37.500%,95.313%,81.771%,91.667%. Pooled versus site-macro differences reflect unequal site counts; no site-level confidence interval is inferred from six/eight small folds. Private receipts retain every held-out query, neighbor patient/site, prediction and site score.

These are **internal site-held-out cross-validation results on selected training-source data**, not prospective validation. Global cohort selection and eligibility used all these source masks, and there was no new untouched test set. The simple sequence result shows that coarse pixels contain strong sequence-label information under this representation and selected cohort. It supports investigating VLM conditioning, objectives and train/development differences; it does not establish the responsible feature, remove protocol shortcuts or demonstrate clinical MRI reasoning. The histogram result specifically cautions against attributing success to anatomy recognition.

The new C2 tumor thumbnail result is higher than prior leave-one-patient-out 18/32, whereas enhancement remains low. A stricter exclusion does not necessarily lower a deterministic nearest-neighbor result because it changes which neighbor is selected. This does not prove site artifacts caused the prior errors or that the selected cohort transports to unseen sites. The earlier same-sample site associations remain distinct from these fitted-fold predictions. Fixed-no remains 16/32 and6/12, a separate constant reference.

## 4. Mask centroid and panel-coordinate audit: actual geometry

The lead reserved an idle window. One small mask plane at a time was extracted; no MRI intensity volume was materialized. The audit covered **68 planes across 38 training-source patients**: all 38 unique C2 frames plus existing training grid-reference frames, with overlap removed. Mask byte hashes, expected 182×218×182 geometry and LAS orientation were checked. Every existing grid label and recorded area reproduced.

For a source-plane voxel index `(i,j)`, `rot90` maps it to panel coordinates with x from i and y from 217−j. Twofold resize maps a pixel center to `(2i+0.5, 2(217−j)+0.5)`. Paired canvas origins are `(4,28)` for FLAIR and `(384,28)` for T1ce. Existing grid labels use panel-local coordinates, not the full 752×470 canvas. They are display locations, not validated patient-anatomical laterality labels. The historical centroid convention omits the 0.5 display-pixel center offset; accounting for it changed **zero grid labels** in the audited ≥1,000-pixel positive set. No target correction is justified by this offset alone.

Eight-connected components, global and largest-component centroids, occupied grid cells and nearest-grid-boundary distances were calculated without modifying labels.

| Audit population | Planes / patients | Tumor≥1,000 pixels | Centroid outside mask | Global vs largest-component grid disagreement | Boundary<10 display pixels | <50% of mask pixels in centroid's grid cell |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All audited references | 68 /38 | 40 | 0 | 0 | 14 | 9 |
| C2 unique frames | 38 /35 | 20 | 0 | 0 | 4 | 2 |
| Preserved grid candidates | 21 /21 | 21 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

The centroid-on-mask check samples the nearest pixel using the declared rounding rule; it is a geometric check, not an expert judgment. Among 21 existing candidates, 14 have one connected component and seven have multiple components; the largest-component fraction is at least 81.49%. Every mask spans multiple grid cells: one spans two, five span three, 14 span four and one spans six. The one candidate with less than half its mask in the centroid cell has 47.48% there. A mathematical centroid can be unambiguous while “where is the lesion?” remains a different visual question. These results do not support declaring a centroid implementation bug; they support retaining an explicitly geometric target and recording the extent ambiguity before task admission.

The preidentified ambiguous-grid frame was inspected as a small unoverlaid paired PNG. It shows broad visible intensity structure and two modality titles; no expert visibility rating or clinical label was assigned. All 38 C2 frames have the same title-band hash and the same bottom-margin hash. The constant headers therefore do not discriminate these binary labels, although image-content, source and normalization shortcuts remain possible. No mask overlays were passed as model inputs.

## 5. Label dependency and task admission

Enhancing pixels are a subset of the total tumor mask by construction. On the 38 unique C2 frames, the paired mask-area states are:

| Total tumor state | Enhancing state | Frames |
|---|---|---:|
| ≥1,000 | ≥1,000 | 6 |
| ≥1,000 | 1–999 | 10 |
| ≥1,000 | 0 | 4 |
| 1–999 | 0 | 1 |
| 0 | 0 | 17 |

The unchanged ≥1,000 threshold and exactly-zero negatives still apply separately to task eligibility. Small positive states remain excluded for the relevant task. These nested targets do not provide two independent pathology capabilities.

**Sequence/contrast task:** source modality labels and paired images are supported, and fixed image features distinguish the classes across held-out sites within the training cohort. A frozen image-dependent endpoint can be exact label accuracy plus patient-pair directional sensitivity. Scanner/protocol shortcut controls and genuinely untouched patient evaluation remain necessary. This is acquisition/contrast discrimination, not a pathology diagnosis.

**Spatial task:** retain the 21 existing ≥1,000-pixel, ≥10-display-pixel-boundary candidates as an engineering reference set. The target must explicitly ask for the projected centroid of the source-defined lesion region in the FLAIR panel. The “largest visible mass,” “majority lesion grid cell,” and “center of all annotated pixels” must not be silently interchanged. Before primary Vero admission, a qualified blind visual assessment must determine whether that source extent is recoverable from the unoverlaid image. A descriptive multi-cell extent endpoint could later be investigated with separately frozen references; it is not implemented or admitted here.

**Current admission decision:** both task families remain `ENGINEERING_ONLY`; source reliability and geometry are improved evidence, but no new expert adjudication or clinical validity is claimed. Do not require identical patient counts across task families or weaken thresholds to fill quotas. Future evaluations should group by patient, report site/category macro and worst-task results, preserve no-image/swap controls, and use the reserved prospective cohort only after protocol freeze under the lead's rules.
